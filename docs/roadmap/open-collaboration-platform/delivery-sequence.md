### Document freshness evidence

Document freshness is `wired` under accepted [ADR-0463](../../decisions/knowledge/documents/0463-refresh-stale-documents-with-evidence-bound-minimal-edits.md) and its [specification](../../specifications/knowledge/document-freshness.md). The resident freshness stage maps ADR-0464 detection to findings, generates candidates through the governed provider path, verifies them deterministically, and reverts with one command. Since [ADR-0561](../../decisions/foundation/0561-decide-without-the-owner-cross-check-reversible-effect-notice.md), Lervo applies every change after its required independent cross-checks agree (two for safety, user-owned statements, and archives), commits it with its reason and verdicts, and reports it as an ADR-0549 notice with the revert command. Fixtures F1 to F7 and the ADR-0561 journeys pass on scratch repositories with the fixture provider. `lervo knowledge freshness apply` and `reject` are removed; held candidates and Lervo debt appear in `lervo knowledge freshness status`, `lervo application attention`, health, and the compact prompt line without owner action.

One verification part remains unmet: no second provider passes no-capability admission yet, so both required rounds run as separate sessions of the same provider; a two-provider fixture run remains open (ADR-0557 follow-up).

### Owner-turn instruction capture evidence

Owner-turn instruction capture is `wired` under [ADR-0467](../../decisions/knowledge/documents/0467-capture-owner-turns-semantically-in-the-knowledge-resident.md) and its [specification](../../specifications/knowledge/instruction-capture.md). The terminal hook and managed conversations queue owner turns without a provider call. The knowledge resident's `instruction_capture` stage assesses them semantically and, after an independent cross-check ([ADR-0561](../../decisions/foundation/0561-decide-without-the-owner-cross-check-reversible-effect-notice.md)), stores candidates that the convergence owner applies, or holds decisions as Lervo debt.

Fixture-provider tests cover English, Korean, paraphrased and implicit corrections, privacy, budget, and cross-provider visibility. A 28-case suite recorded 1.000 class agreement against Claude. Explicit intake succeeds on the reproduced English and Korean requests.

Remaining boundaries:

- No installed run on the Lervo repository has converted a real Claude Code or Discord owner turn into a reviewed candidate.
- Conversations use the queue rather than the ADR-0457 `knowledgeCandidate` turn field.
- Cross-host exchange is tested against a fake Hub and the real Hub route, but has not run between the installed Mac and WSL hosts.

Each host assesses its own owner turns. On Hub-coordinated hosts, only the ADR-0466 convergence owner applies and commits a candidate. A standalone host applies locally without the Hub commit requirement.

### Owner-free layout, migration, and structure effects

Under [ADR-0561](../../decisions/foundation/0561-decide-without-the-owner-cross-check-reversible-effect-notice.md) slice A3, repository layout, continuation migration, canonical instruction bootstrap and bridge, document normalization, and document structure changes are `wired` without an owner step. The background lifecycle reviewer applies a reviewed plan whatever the delegation's roots, so a plan with paths far beyond the 32-root limit applies. A foreground `--apply` asks a fresh no-capability session for the same exact plan review first. Each effect lands as its own `Lervo Knowledge` commit with `Lervo-Effect` trailers, the learned notice reports it, and `lervo knowledge revert <commit>` undoes it; a reverted layout move stays in place until its bytes change. A rejected plan is parked as Lervo debt. `--approve-item` for these stages and the ADR-0559 `--accept-layout` and `--decline-layout` commands are removed. Runtime and CLI tests cover agreement, disagreement, a 20-move plan across many roots, commit-then-delete, and revert.

Remaining boundaries:

- Not yet observed on argo after release: the layout moves applying with no owner command, followed by a notice with the undo command.
- The terminal integration `--approve-item` and `lervo upgrade --approval` remain for later slices.

### Owner-free canonicalization, delegation, and continuity

Under [ADR-0561](../../decisions/foundation/0561-decide-without-the-owner-cross-check-reversible-effect-notice.md) slice A4, canonicalization candidates, delegation renewal, continuity findings, and turn declarations are `wired` without an owner step:

- Only the background canonical reviewer resolves a candidate. A deferral regenerates the review once, then holds the candidate as Lervo debt while convergence continues. `lervo knowledge canonicalization review` and `promote` are removed, and `reopen` undoes a decision.
- A delegation outside the policy limits renews narrowed to them, and the resident rolls a near policy horizon forward with the same limits.
- Continuity `owner_decision` findings become `lervo_debt`, which never requires user action.
- The delegation `scope-response` command and its proposal, decision, and response records are removed.
- New declarations cannot claim `pending_user_approval`. Remaining lifecycle work is declared `pending_background`, and `turn_approval_pending` is no longer reported.
- A captured owner decision enters canon through the cross-checked rule path. A captured rule the cross-check rejects regenerates once before it is held as Lervo debt.
- A source its generator marks `needs_review` regenerates once before it is parked as Lervo debt.

Runtime and CLI tests cover each replacement: deferral regeneration and hold, narrowed renewal, horizon roll-forward, legacy continuity state, decision capture, capture regeneration, `needs_review` regeneration, and the removed commands.

Remaining boundaries:

- Not yet observed on an installed host after release: a renewal narrowed to the policy limits, a rolled horizon, or a held candidate shown as Lervo debt.
- Owner-required attention that remains belongs to later slices or to credentials: Hub ownership verdicts and replica divergence (A6), and `gh` authentication for knowledge publication (credentials, notice-only).

### Owner-free workstream, replica, and supervision decisions

Under [ADR-0561](../../decisions/foundation/0561-decide-without-the-owner-cross-check-reversible-effect-notice.md) slice A6, three owner waits are `wired` without an owner step:

- **Workstream heads.** Divergent local and Hub heads reconcile to the newest valid head. The other head stays on the Hub, readable by its exact hash, and `lervo workstream reconcile --restore <snapshot-hash>` restores it. Nodes reconcile on their own sync cycle, and `--keep`, `--apply`, and `--write` are removed.
- **Replica conflicts.** The coordinator saves the losing side to a `lervo/replica-backup` branch, confirms with two agreeing checks, and then prefers the configured side. `--prefer` is removed, and an open divergence is Lervo debt.
- **Supervision escalation.** Past the escalation bound, the principal proxy settles within the installed operating mandate or holds the goal as `lervo_debt`.

Sync, Hub, node-runtime, orchestrator, work-controller, and CLI tests cover each replacement.

Remaining boundaries:

- Not yet observed on the installed Mac and WSL fleet after release. The Hub must deploy before the Nodes for the exact revision read; until then, divergence stays held.
- Node health still shows supervision Lervo debt as `supervision_attention_required` with reason codes `lervo_debt` and the reason, not as a dedicated debt line.
