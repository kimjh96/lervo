# Acceptance gates

Checked gates have implementation evidence and focused tests. Shared provider contracts do not establish a successful native Claude journey. Unchecked compound gates retain these unobserved portions: all installed producer surfaces, capacity/process-death handoff, complete asset policy coverage, concurrent live-writer and dirty-target journeys, all journal/integration crash windows, and installed response latency.

## Gate groups

- [Source capture and baseline currency](acceptance/source-capture.md)
- [Execution evidence and durable continuation](acceptance/execution-evidence.md)
- [Independent review and canonical context](acceptance/independent-review.md)
- [Integration, ownership, and resource limits](acceptance/integration.md)
- [Installed concurrency and canary verification](acceptance/installed-concurrency.md)
- [Maintenance recovery and speed](../maintenance-recovery-and-speed.md): first delivery is installed on Mac and WSL, and the reader-compatibility gate is checked. Complete evidence recovery, freshness fairness, exact assessment settlement, safe-effect protocols, paired performance measurements, and seven-day installed acceptance remain open.
