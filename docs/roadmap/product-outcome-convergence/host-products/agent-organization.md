# Multi-team agent organization

Part of [Paperclip-, Buzz-, loop-, graph-, and host-product outcomes](../host-products.md).

State: `delivery required`.

## Outcome

One human owner operates a Lervo organization with central leadership and several repository-backed project teams. Each hired expert agent retains one durable identity, exact role history, provider-neutral context, one-to-one external bot identity, explicit channel membership, and scoped authority. A central CEO agent coordinates teams and performs reviewed organization and Discord administration within policy.

## Current foundations

Implemented foundations include workforce identity, roles, assignments, handoffs, workstreams, approvals, bounded scheduling and graphs, provider-neutral deliberation, per-bot Discord deployments, exact agent and role hashes in conversation records, and bounded agent-specific recent conversation input. These foundations do not establish the complete organization outcome.

## Completion criteria

1. Durable organization and project-team membership contracts.
2. CEO policy, budget, delegation, approval, and Discord administration adapters.
3. An agent-context namespace with source-bound promotion, projection rebuild, retirement, and successor handoff.
4. Natural addressed routing for participants, roles, teams, and bounded discussion groups.
5. One installed scenario with a human, CEO, and at least two project teams whose agents collaborate without crossing authority or context boundaries.
6. One real Discord channel with two distinct installed agent bots completing a bounded deliberation and stopping at its terminal condition.
