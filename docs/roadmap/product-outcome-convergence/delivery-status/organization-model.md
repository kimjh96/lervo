# Organization model

The accepted model distinguishes organizations, repository-backed project teams, role contracts, durable hired expert agents, provider turns, one-to-one external speaker identities, channel membership, and bounded discussions. Discord speakers may use bot users or exact webhook sets over a shared authenticated Gateway.

Conversation records preserve exact hired-agent and role hashes. Unique display identity remains separate from role and provider. Agent-specific recent turns feed managed responses; response failure retries terminate visibly.

Implemented outcomes include durable organization membership, central/project team reclassification, natural Discord channel and thread effects in the Node runtime (ADR-0560 removed the CLI executive effects), and bounded deliberation-outcome reconciliation.

Repository product-owner role contracts and durable named PO identities can be installed independently of Discord deployment. The following remain `delivery required` until live evidence proves them:

- Natural team-scoped PO hiring and assignment effects.
- Approval effects.
- Reviewed accumulated agent-context promotion through real turns.
- Shared-general-channel group routing.
- Installed multi-team acceptance.
