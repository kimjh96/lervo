# Delivery sequence

1. Version mandate admission and responsibility bindings. Add exact automated policy review alongside manual topology review. Verify ancestry, capacity, revocation, and live-lease changes.
2. Ground requests and workforce planning in existing context and search. Compare reuse, reassignment, owner execution, borrowing, and hiring. Preflight workers against actual task requirements.
3. Connect work events to serialized owner decisions. Reuse idle-reconciliation wakeups; add durable deadlines and replay. Verify recovery after actual owner-process termination, exclusion of a live expired owner, old-token fencing, and preservation of unresolved effects and reservations. Do not add a full-history poller or modify the concurrent replica implementation.
4. Connect start confirmation, failure recovery, rework, acceptance, and separate user delivery. Bind conversation, native, child, and retry causality. Expose incomplete telemetry. Verify canonical cancellation through the snapshot and transport schemas, unstarted assignment cleanup after expiry or revocation, restart deduplication, event-failure rollback, and preservation of expired leases and unsettled runs.
5. Connect central escalation and cross-team acceptance through scoped handoffs. Bind deployments to responsibilities independently of names or chat topology.
6. Before deployment activation, verify local headless and Discord journeys. Install the repository-file research source policy and exact artifact checker. Prove independently evaluated research acceptance through the [research execution contract](../../../../../specifications/execution/outcome-supervision/implementation/research-execution.md); deterministic provenance alone cannot complete research. AXLI's divergent histories require a verified authoritative execution side or separate reconciliation before cross-device history claims.

## Acceptance across stages

For existing long-lived workstreams, every stage must verify the [reviewed goal scope](../../../../../specifications/execution/outcome-supervision/contracts/work-scope.md):

- Preserve the exact predecessor.
- Reject active or unsettled prior work.
- Account for the complete new causal tree.
- Prevent budget resets through mandate succession.
- Retain exact prior responsibility bindings without recreating assignments.
- Accept only bindings recorded before successor installation.
- Apply current effect authority.

Worker-readiness acceptance must use a repository whose full knowledge inspection takes more than five seconds. Verify the separate bounded knowledge window, unchanged short-lived authority and lease checks, freshly loaded task sources, and rejection of expired or changed knowledge. See [knowledge readiness](../../../../../specifications/execution/outcome-supervision/implementation/knowledge-readiness.md).

Verify reservation-only startup recovery against the exact installed CLI research checker. Preserve the sole reservation and all prior usage; require current-owner fencing. Reject any requested run, additional event, substituted reservation, or late event after supersession. CLI upgrades must refresh the checker binding before actual research acceptance.

Research acceptance must verify default-versus-explicit model accounting under ADR-0400. Retain exact reporting groups and all historical usage. Prove complete model and counter equivalence before deriving the mandate total; reject mixed or missing evidence. Continue independent review of the existing successful artifact without repeating the worker.
