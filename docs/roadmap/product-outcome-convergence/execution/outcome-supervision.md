# Outcome supervision delivery

State: `delivery required`
As of: 2026-09-11

The Lervo execution application owns acceptance: a natural-language goal produces reviewed artifacts or a precise authority decision without repeated manual hiring, dispatch, failure inspection, or recovery prompts. Product owners autonomously define worker responsibilities within an installed mandate. Central coordination handles ownership, cross-team support, and shared blockers. Existing organization, scheduling, and handoff foundations do not establish this outcome.

## Completion boundary

Design completion requires reviewable linked contracts and documentation/knowledge checks. Implementation additionally requires structural review, relevant tests, common provider contracts, quality checks, task-base release completion with reviewed intent, installed scenarios, and knowledge refresh. Deployment acceptance is separate from design acceptance.
