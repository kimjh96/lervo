# Natural-language grounding quality

Status: `active`
Started: 2026-08-27

## Objective

Validate practical Korean and mixed-language repository requests while separating search ranking, conversational grounding, approval authority, and evidence of use. Production uses typed same-provider semantic grounding. Lexical scoring retrieves candidates and may precede bounded autonomous inspection. Rejected experiments have no routing authority.

## Evaluation contract

| Layer                      | Input                                                                                     | Required output                                                                                            | Activation authority           |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Engine retrieval           | One deterministic query and reviewed expected paths                                       | Top-10 success, recall, reciprocal rank, and per-task comparison                                           | None; observation only         |
| Conversational grounding   | Prompt, bounded prior turns, workstream availability, authority, and repository condition | Separate intent, reference, source-class, disposition, required-behavior, and prohibited-behavior verdicts | None; observation only         |
| Managed execution evidence | Canonical run and context events                                                          | Selected, loaded, injected, referenced, and missing stages without inferred use                            | Existing execution policy only |

The matrix contains 20 Korean-framed cases and 20 one-to-one English controls across four task types, exactly five per type, plus 32 grounding cases across eight categories, exactly four per category. Coverage includes positive, ambiguous, abstention, stale-binding, no-source, and unreviewed-relation cases; context-dependent dispositions for identical prompts; public fixture text; digest-only runtime events; and report redaction.

Initial suites remain `verification_authored` and `pending_human_review`; they cannot authorize hybrid activation or establish production representativeness.

## Current state

Qualified Lexical v2 is the shipped default for repositories without an explicit reviewed local selection ([ADR-0328](../decisions/search/engines/0328-make-qualified-lexical-v2-the-product-default.md), [ADR-0413](../decisions/search/engines/0413-close-product-default-lexical-v2-surface-gaps.md)). Historical Lexical v1 results remain the local rollback baseline.

Operational search remained `lexical-v1` through the experiments below. `local-hybrid-v1`, bounded fallback v1, and assisted v2-v7 were rejected. V8 met quality, evidence, and non-token criteria but failed the token-ratio gate because control usage was incomplete. Unchanged v9 passed every preregistered criterion without activation. [ADR-0248](../decisions/search/0248-stage-linked-evidence-reconciliation-activation.md) authorized only effect-free managed-run phase-A shadow projection.

The isolated phase-A [pilot v3](../evidence/search/linked-evidence-shadow-phase-a-registered-pilot-v3.md) completed all 30 eligible samples: 30 immutable observations, 24 nonempty projections, exact replay, no privacy failures, exactly 60 provider invocations, and unchanged primary effects. Provider shadow and production effects remained unauthorized.

The [v18 four-case relational confirmation](../evidence/search/provider-shadow/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-pilot-v18.md) completed 32/32 fresh cross-provider source-selection calls without retry. Both providers retained 100% recall in every observation; mean precision was 93.75% for Codex and 76.04% for Claude.

### Managed-context canaries

The following canary and shadow chronology is historical. The [execution specification](../specifications/execution.md) identifies their removal under [ADR-0528](../decisions/search/activation/0528-remove-the-linked-evidence-canary-and-shadow.md). Historical activation and default-off statements below describe those experiments, not current feature availability.

[ADR-0277](../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/0277-stage-managed-context-relational-canary.md) established a default-off canary and authorized only implementation and provider-free verification. Live traffic, managed-context effects, direct-terminal changes, operational-search changes, and production activation required an exact prepared checkpoint over an eligible registered corpus. Provider-free fixtures proved the one-turn JSON Schema contract, exact selected-to-loaded-to-injected evidence inputs, owner-only bounded records, conflicting-replay rejection, and zero adapter creations for ineligible coordinates. They did not prove native Codex tool isolation.

[ADR-0278](../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/0278-prepare-exact-managed-context-canary-checkpoint.md) prepared eight reviewed source tasks paired across eight Codex and eight Claude runs, with at most one selection and one task call per run. Its owner-only manifest bound exact recommendation, source-snapshot, projection, schema, model, and rollback coordinates. Preparation used zero calls and retained false execution/effect authority; live execution required operator authorization of the final manifest digest.

After authorization, [canary v1](../evidence/search/provider-shadow/linked-evidence-managed-canary-v1.md) stopped on its first Codex selection call: the read-only adapter emitted a forbidden command-execution signal. No selection completed, managed task or Claude call started, or source changed. [ADR-0279](../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/0279-stop-managed-context-canary-v1-on-command-signal.md) rejected v1 without retry. Effective tool, command, and file-change capabilities must all be unsupported before candidate injection or a model turn. Provider-free tests stop the current Codex app-server with zero selection turns and permit an empty-tool Claude session.

[ADR-0280](../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/0280-add-an-opt-in-codex-responses-no-tool-transport.md) added an opt-in Codex Responses transport with no tools, `tool_choice: "none"`, no storage, and the exact selection schema. Ordinary Codex execution stayed on app-server. [ADR-0281](../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/0281-prepare-managed-context-canary-v2.md) retained this design in a non-executable 32-call checkpoint; its separate API credential was not a product activation prerequisite.

[ADR-0282](../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/0282-remove-api-key-requirement-from-managed-context-canary.md) removed API keys from the primary experiment path. Provider-free v3 preparation correctly bound passed V18 evidence but retained no added source for Codex and only one for Claude. [V3](../evidence/search/provider-shadow/linked-evidence-managed-canary-v3.md) was rejected before execution with zero calls.

[ADR-0283](../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/0283-prepare-deterministic-linked-context-canary-v4.md) proved every paired run had nonempty deterministic added context. [ADR-0284](../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/0284-prepare-exact-deterministic-native-session-canary-v5.md) bound the schema-v2 runtime. [ADR-0285](../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/0285-authorize-exact-key-free-managed-canary-v6.md) added exact authorization, attempt-before-call persistence, and fail-first native-session execution. Selection calls remained capped at zero and total task calls at 16. V1-v5 remained immutable. Live paired measurement required the exact final v6 manifest digest and no API key.

[Canary v11](../evidence/search/provider-shadow/linked-evidence-managed-canary-v11.md) completed eight Codex and eight Claude native-session runs without technical failures, retries, selection calls, API keys, or repository changes. Both achieved source recall 1.0. Codex passed reference-precision gates; Claude mean precision was 0.5014, with three runs below 0.5 because E4 output also marked operational and adjacent context. Activation remained default-off.

[ADR-0291](../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/0291-constrain-provider-neutral-e4-references-for-managed-canary-v12.md) authorized only a provider-neutral request-side constraint and fresh v12 paired confirmation. Post-filtering observed references and disclosing expected source identifiers were forbidden. [V12](../evidence/search/provider-shadow/linked-evidence-managed-canary-v12.md) completed all 16 fresh runs without technical failure. Claude mean precision rose to 65.97%, but two runs remained below 50%. Codex mean precision rose to 91.67%, but two runs each lost one expected source, lowering mean recall to 87.5%. [ADR-0292](../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/0292-reject-prompt-only-e4-constraint-after-managed-canary-v12.md) rejected and rolled back the prompt-only correction.

[ADR-0293](../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/0293-type-task-evidence-and-governance-context.md) completed the provider-neutral typed-delivery boundary and common Codex-Claude tests without provider calls. [V14](../evidence/search/provider-shadow/linked-evidence-managed-canary-v14.md) completed 16/16 typed runs with full recall for both providers and no technical failure. Codex passed every accuracy gate; Claude mean precision was 60.07%, with two runs below 50%. [ADR-0296](../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/typed-context/0296-reject-activation-after-typed-context-managed-canary-v14.md) rejected activation. [ADR-0297](../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/typed-context/0297-separate-claim-reference-eligibility-from-task-evidence.md) recorded the provider-neutral diagnosis and added independent claim eligibility while preserving linked supporting context. Activation still required provider-free verification and a fresh paired canary; the feature remained default-off.

[ADR-0298](../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/typed-context/0298-stop-v15-on-measurement-projection-failure.md) preserved v15 after one post-turn measurement failure and no accepted accuracy sample. The correction exposed only sorted, unique canonical E4 document IDs in the existing bounded run summary. Fresh source-current v16 had to pass the unchanged 8+8 gates before activation.

[ADR-0299](../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/typed-context/0299-stop-v16-and-expand-canary-observation-capacity.md) preserved v16 after 11 accepted results and one post-turn storage-capacity failure. Its 12 task calls and zero selection calls produced an incomplete population that could not support activation. The bounded owner-only observation cap increased from 64 to 128 without deleting historical evidence. Fresh source-current v17 had to pass the unchanged 8+8 gates.

[ADR-0300](../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/typed-context/0300-stop-v17-and-expand-complete-event-capacity.md) preserved v17 after 10 accepted results and one pre-provider complete-event source failure. V17 used 10 task calls and zero selection calls; the failed run made no provider call. The complete execution-event count cap increased from 4,096 to 8,192; the 16 MiB byte cap and privacy validation stayed unchanged. No event was deleted or truncated. Fresh source-current v18 had to pass the unchanged 8+8 gates.

[Canary v18](../evidence/search/provider-shadow/linked-evidence-managed-canary-v18.md) completed eight Codex and eight Claude source-current runs with zero technical failures, selection calls, retries, API keys, or repository mutations. Both achieved 100% recall; mean precision was 83.33% for Codex and 79.17% for Claude, with every run at or above 50%. [ADR-0301](../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/typed-context/0301-activate-deterministic-claim-eligible-linked-context.md) activated bounded deterministic linked supporting context for ordinary managed runs without a selection adapter or user configuration.

### Retirement and terminal grounding

The [lexical-only versus linked-context paired evaluation](../evidence/search/paired-uplift/lexical-linked-uplift-v1.md) found no incremental quality uplift across 32 Codex and Claude runs. Expected-source recall stayed at 100%; linked context reduced equal-provider mean claim recall and complete-answer rate by 6.25 percentage points each. Mean duration fell 9.89%, Claude total tokens rose 28.33%, and five task pairs lost quality versus two wins. The source worktree had no registered search documents, so ordinary linked projection was empty. [ADR-0302](../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/typed-context/0302-retire-linked-default-and-use-search-engine-ports.md) retired the ordinary linked default and restored lexical-only operation. At that stage the exact canary remained default-off; the execution specification now records removal of the canary and shadow under ADR-0528. The evaluation history remains historical evidence.

[ADR-0305](../decisions/execution/grounding/0305-bind-terminal-grounding-to-native-structured-output.md) corrected typed provider grounding without lexical routing. [ADR-0327](../decisions/execution/providers/hooks/0327-availability-safe-unmanaged-terminal-hooks.md) later removed that effect-grounding call from direct unmanaged prompt preparation. Under [ADR-0531](../decisions/execution/providers/hooks/0531-bound-terminal-hooks-and-retain-degraded-context.md), both providers use compact preparation by default. `LERVO_TERMINAL_CONTEXT=retrieval` preserves the opt-in provider-neutral projection and retrieval path under the same deadlines. Preparation grants no effect or authority and materializes no durable rule. Terminal selection and loading claims derive only from observed selected documents.

ADR-0531 gives the shared hook one 15,000 ms budget. Preparation failure returns the AGENTS.md pointer and a short closed degraded reason. Independently bounded local reads may retain publication policy, complete applied owner clauses, and the verified recorded workstream headline; workstream freshness remains uninspected. The degraded response omits workstream next actions, blockers, decisions and approvals, knowledge health, freshness counts, recommendations and retrieved documents. It creates no new context-prepared event or activation receipt for missing snapshot evidence and proves no provider delivery. Failure recording is best effort when storage is unavailable. Installed latency remains a separate verification gate.

See [evaluation history](natural-language-grounding-quality/evaluation-history.md) for detailed immutable failures, observations, measurements, and delivery chronology.

## Current retrieval work

The first [lexical-v2 local and provider comparison](../evidence/search/lexical-v2/lexical-v2-local-and-provider-comparison-v1.md) improved local aggregate ranking and warm-query latency, but missed the existing per-task quality threshold and built about 2.3 times slower. Codex preserved answer pass count and recall but used 0.55% more reported tokens and 9.26% more time, with slightly lower precision. Claude result framing left usage and quality incomplete. [ADR-0307](../decisions/search/engines/0307-revise-lexical-v2-after-local-and-provider-evaluation.md) rejected activation and separated common structured output, section-scoped evidence efficiency, and multilingual retrieval work.

The strengthened [v12 confirmation](../evidence/search/lexical-v2/lexical-v2-activation-confirmation-v12.md) passed augmented English and Korean local-quality gates, preserved all original cases, and reduced candidate evidence bytes to 37.65% of baseline. A final Korean rerun exceeded the unchanged build-p95 cost bound. Codex improved tokens, recall, and precision but failed one new case and the unchanged duration ratio. Claude improved measured efficiency and retrieval metrics but failed the common zero-tool contract because native structured output appeared as tool activity. [ADR-0309](../decisions/search/engines/0309-retain-lexical-v1-after-lexical-v2-v12-confirmation.md) retained lexical-v1. A successor required newly preregistered confirmation, not a retry or weaker gate.

After retaining a v19 Codex duration failure, [v20 confirmation](../evidence/search/lexical-v2/lexical-v2-activation-confirmation-v20.md) increased the preregistered sample to five repetitions and proportionally raised the observation floor. From one matching suite and corpus digest, Codex and Claude each completed 120 calls without retries. Both candidates passed 60/60 observations and all 12 cases every repetition, with 100% recall, at least 90.83% precision, no provider, contract, or tool failures, and unchanged token, duration, and byte gates. [ADR-0318](../decisions/search/engines/0318-qualify-lexical-v2-after-v20-cross-provider-confirmation.md) made the build eligible for authorized activation review without synthesizing human review or repository transition. `lexical-v1` remained the no-transition default and rollback engine.

The exact four-query [reviewed real-use suite](../../benchmarks/search/lexical-v2-real-use-reviewed-v1.json) binds explicit user approval and cannot be trimmed or replaced. After the ADR-0319 failure, a provider-neutral projection port and deterministic independent-query fusion corrected the boundary without query-specific aliases or weaker thresholds. Fresh five-repetition runs passed lexical-v2 20/20 for Codex and 20/20 for Claude on the same 455-document corpus; all usage was observed and no projection failed. ADR-0320 accepted that retrieval evidence without manufacturing an engine transition. [ADR-0321](../decisions/search/engines/0321-activate-lexical-v2-through-retrieval-plan-selection.md) closes the gap through a content-bound complete-plan bundle, independent immutable selection and rollback chain, current-corpus validation, selected-engine execution, and fail-closed terminal routing.

[ADR-0353](../decisions/execution/providers/hooks/0353-remove-redundant-terminal-query-admission.md) removed the result-free admission model turn before each direct-terminal query projection. The retrieval lifecycle opens one isolated provider runtime for the actual projection, reports it as `query_projection`, and preserves the provider-neutral schema and retrieval boundary. Fresh source-current reviewed runs passed 20/20 for each provider. The terminal receipt binds the sole pre-search model turn instead of omitting admission usage from measured projection cost. The superseded admitted lifecycle remains identifiable only for historical evidence and compatibility. Under ADR-0531 this retrieval path is opt-in; it is not the compact default, and slow retrieval can degrade at the shared deadline without an additional provider call.

## Context quality and surface parity

The local terminal correction prepares the configured English publication instruction independently of retrieval; installed writing compliance and general Hit uplift remain unverified. The [context-quality delivery roadmap](natural-language-grounding-quality/context-quality-parity.md) preserves the reliability findings and owns shared contracts, module boundaries, performance evaluation and completion criteria for Codex/Claude terminals, Discord and a future first-party host under ADR-0501. Source implementation now includes request/plan validation, local preparation, task and writing observations, bounded correction, host composition and reader-first event support. Complete corpus/token observations, reviewed real-task quality, recording-resource qualification and installed acceptance remain open.

## Current boundaries

Retain current ports for deterministic single-engine compatibility. A bounded `retrieve -> optional fuse -> optional rerank` plan above them supports `lexical-v2`, external-only replacement, local-plus-external augmentation, and external reranking without hidden stages or another post-search exception. Candidate registration is inert. Activation requires paired quality, latency, privacy, and cost evidence with explicit rollback to `lexical-v1` ([ADR-0306](../decisions/search/engines/0306-separate-search-engine-replacement-and-composition.md)).

The `bm25f-v1` control was removed by [ADR-0560](../decisions/foundation/0560-remove-unused-subsystems-to-harden-the-core.md) after Lexical v2 became the product default. The first `lexical-v2` experiment combines field-aware Unicode word ranking, identifier-aware splitting, character n-gram retrieval, and deterministic rank fusion. Before implementing an external replacement prototype, compare all three local arms on the same current corpus and reviewed Korean, English, mixed-language, exact-identifier, morphology, multi-source, ambiguous-neighbor, and no-hit cases. Augmentation and reranking are separate later candidates; reranking cannot repair first-stage recall.

No stage may:

- Treat shadow-selected, loaded, injected, hook-prepared, or output-referenced bodies as provider use.
- Add fixture-specific term limits or fixed sources.
- Rely on provider completeness declarations.
- Introduce question-category routing.
- Relax thresholds or infer completeness from confidence.
- Silently activate managed provider calls.

Human-reviewed ground truth is required for provenance promotion, production-quality claims, or activation. Human review does not gate negative diagnosis or ordinary engineering work without bulk fixture approval.

## Completion criteria

- Every suite passes the repository validator and focused negative tests.
- Paired reports bind the same corpus digest and disclose the English-dominant corpus limit.
- Human-reviewed cases contain real lexical failures for every task type; synthetic success cannot substitute.
- Grounding reports intent, reference, source class, and disposition separately.
- Managed ambiguous references, missing workstreams, stale hashes, absent authority, no sources, and unreviewed graph relations fail closed without invented paths or rationale. Direct root-session lifecycle gaps remain observable but do not block provider completion.
- Invalid provider grounding never selects a workflow, effect, capability, authority, provider, or source. Managed consumers reject the failure.
- Direct Codex and Claude hooks use compact preparation by default. Opt-in retrieval uses provider-neutral current-repository query projection without effect grounding, keyword, alias, translation, or phrase routing. Empty or failed local retrieval never supports an absence claim about another repository or service. Preparation failure returns bounded degraded context under ADR-0531, without new context-prepared or activation evidence for missing snapshot evidence. Stop lifecycle blocking, rejected ingress, and unexpected-error fallbacks retain ADR-0531's distinct rules.
- When opt-in direct-terminal query projection runs, its single pre-search provider model turn is covered by its receipt and `query_projection` timing. Historical `projection_admission` is a versioned compatibility label, not a current production stage. Deadline degradation adds no provider call and cannot claim a completed projection without evidence.
- Search, graph recommendation, grounding quality, and managed execution evidence remain separate contracts.
- Retrieval plans expose every retrieve, fusion, and rerank stage with exact adapter, configuration, input, bound, result, privacy, duration, and failure coordinates. No stage is hidden inside another adapter.
- Before every terminal retrieval, an activated plan revalidates its tracked bundle, current corpus snapshot and digest, projector population, retriever build, fusion policy, and rollback target. Any mismatch fails closed.
- External-only, augmentation, and reranking modes have distinct reviewed plan identities. Required-stage failure never triggers silent omission, engine mixing, stale-index use, or fallback.
- Third-party search adapters are installed and content-locked out of process. Queries never dynamically load arbitrary modules, paths, packages, or URLs.
- Historical empty default projections preserved the ordinary context plan, and default and exact-canary deterministic paths created no selection adapter. These are historical evaluation properties; ADR-0528 removal leaves no current canary activation criterion here.
- No result upgrades selected, loaded, injected, referenced, or used beyond observed telemetry.
- Relevant tests, quality checks, documentation lifecycle, knowledge refresh, and release-completion review pass.
