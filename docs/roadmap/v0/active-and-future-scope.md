# Separate active and future scope

These scopes do not reopen v0 acceptance; each follows its linked ADR and roadmap.

- [Product outcome convergence](../product-outcome-convergence.md): truth ledger for outcomes, evidence, and gaps.
- [Foundation hardening](../foundation-hardening.md): completed event compatibility, causal work, resource evidence, adverse-condition proof, and enhancement handoff.
- [Governed loop and assignment graph](../governed-loop-assignment-graph.md): completed bounded headless decisions and the packed product-host protocol over canonical assignments and managed evidence, with no second task or graph authority.
- [Extensible retrieval](../../decisions/search/engines/0328-make-qualified-lexical-v2-the-product-default.md): qualified lexical-v2 and its reviewed projection plan are the repository-independent default. [Surface convergence](../../decisions/search/engines/0413-close-product-default-lexical-v2-surface-gaps.md) closes prior CLI, managed-work, supervision, fallback, and conversation gaps while preserving explicit reviewed alternatives. External composition remains behind provider-neutral boundaries.
- Gemini and Copilot bridges.
- Knowledge follow-up: [branch-safe lifecycle](../knowledge/branch-safe-lifecycle.md) and [normalization renewal](../knowledge/document-normalization-renewal.md).
- [Open collaboration platform](../open-collaboration-platform.md): ADR-0452 reverses closed defaults for conversation, memory, learning, and autonomy, checks authority at consequential effects, defines completion labels and the six true-Lervo journeys, and sequences ADR-0453 to ADR-0459.
- [Autonomous background convergence](../knowledge/autonomous-background-convergence.md): local implementation and a private installed no-Hub canary verify independent canonical review, normalization application, baseline debt recovery, and knowledge currency without a later user turn; full installed acceptance and fleet activation remain open.
- External graph databases, embeddings, or Hub semantic services.
- [Organization deployment and fleet contracts](../operations/organization-runtime-credential-sources.md) are complete; external authorities, unattended automation, and publication remain future.
