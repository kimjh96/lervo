# Grounding and retrieval

- Managed context grounding and coverage: per-document E1–E4 evidence; every selection ground; separate delivery, reference, and relevance coverage; explicit managed and unmanaged boundaries without claiming understanding.
- Local hybrid search and reviewed relation context: historically registered deterministic keyword-plus-sparse-embedding candidates; top-50 and reviewed top-10 contracts; reproducible 20-case quality and latency observation. Optimized build and warm-query costs passed, but the historical quality gates were unresolved and activation was undetermined. [ADR-0226](../../../decisions/search/0226-retire-local-hybrid-and-evaluate-bounded-model-fallback.md) subsequently retired `local-hybrid-v1`, its registration, and the dedicated benchmark command; the measurements remain historical evidence. Activation of future candidates requires measured need. Snapshot-bound, sidecar-bound one-hop graph recommendations used ordinary E2/E3 delivery until [ADR-0523](../../../decisions/search/activation/0523-retire-reviewed-relation-context.md) retired them, because no reviewed relation ever reached the projection.
- Claim-eligible linked supporting context: completed the default-off canary, typed claim-authority boundary, and fresh 8+8 activation population. A lexical-only control found no quality uplift, so ADR-0302 retired the ordinary default while preserving historical evidence.
