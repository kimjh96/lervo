# Natural-language grounding evaluation history

This history covers retrieval, grounding, Phase A, provider-shadow, managed-canary, and paired uplift evaluations. Operational search remains `lexical-v1`. ADR-0302 retired the ADR-0301 ordinary linked default after the paired uplift result failed to establish an accuracy or cross-provider token benefit. The exact canary remains default-off historical infrastructure.
