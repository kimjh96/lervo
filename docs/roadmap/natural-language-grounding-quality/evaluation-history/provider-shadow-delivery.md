# Provider-shadow and managed-canary delivery

## Provider-shadow pilots v1-v9

1. [ADR-0253](../../decisions/search/activation/0253-pre-register-effect-free-provider-shadow-pilot.md) authorized 24 calls. [V1](../../evidence/search/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-pilot-v1.md) reproduced the 24-sample, 16-candidate, and 130,302-byte bounds, then stopped after one call. Delivery mapped `sourceId` to `documentId`, but parsing read `documentId` from the original records and rejected every token as unknown. No canonical observation persisted. A corrected post-stop replay selected exactly two reviewed relevant sources with 100% recall and precision, but lacked shadow-run, local-session, target-turn, adapter-receipt, and duration coordinates, so it could not replace the immutable record. Primary-effect digests were unchanged.
2. A successor required a reviewed contract and authority, preservation of stopped v1, one candidate identity shape, bounded unavailable records for every completed rejected turn, refusal after failure, and a fresh population without retries or borrowed results. Passage could authorize only a separate canary-design decision. [ADR-0254](../../decisions/search/activation/0254-pre-register-recoverable-provider-shadow-pilot-v2.md) was superseded before execution when Claude measurement became required; ADR-0255 replaced it.
3. [ADR-0255](../../decisions/search/activation/0255-authorize-paired-codex-claude-provider-shadow-v2.md) prepared 24 pairs and stopped after one valid Codex and one unavailable Claude observation. Codex selected both reviewed sources with 100% precision and recall in 11.438 seconds and reduced selected-body bytes by 90.28%. Claude completed but failed the aggregate-result boundary; the cause was not retained. See [v2](../../evidence/search/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-pilot-v2.md).
4. [ADR-0256](../../decisions/search/activation/0256-correct-claude-provider-shadow-observability-v3.md) removed Claude tools and separated bounded result failures. The first Claude turn lacked provider-neutral usage because the adapter discarded SDK `modelUsage`; no Codex call was made. See [v3](../../evidence/search/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-pilot-v3.md). [ADR-0257](../../decisions/search/activation/0257-observe-claude-sdk-usage-provider-shadow-v4.md) added tested cumulative-usage normalization with reviewed `patch` intent. V4 passed usage but failed strict E4-only grammar. Across v2-v4, four calls occurred; fail-closed stopping avoided 47 of 51. No valid cross-provider speed, accuracy, token, or cost comparison exists. See the [final report](../../evidence/search/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-pilot-v4.md).
5. [ADR-0258](../../decisions/search/activation/0258-correct-claude-structured-selection-provider-shadow-v5.md) retained legacy grammar, added opt-in Claude SDK JSON Schema output, and authorized three fail-first calls. V5 stopped after one turn because the unavailable validator rejected the new coordinate prefix. No exact outcome survived; v5 is immutable and evidence-ineligible. See [v5](../../evidence/search/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-pilot-v5.md).
6. [ADR-0259](../../decisions/search/activation/0259-preserve-claude-selection-failure-provider-shadow-v6.md) persisted bounded outcomes before observation construction and authorized three fresh calls. V6 stopped after classifying its first turn `provider_shadow_tool_activity`: the SDK used an end-turn tool carrier for JSON Schema although no ordinary Claude tools were exposed. See [v6](../../evidence/search/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-pilot-v6.md).
7. [ADR-0260](../../decisions/search/activation/0260-classify-claude-structured-output-framing-provider-shadow-v7.md) permitted only one provider-native output-framing event and kept commands and other tools fail-closed. V7 produced three eligible Claude observations with 100% recall and complete measurements, but 52.22% precision failed the 65% gate because of redundancy. See [v7](../../evidence/search/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-pilot-v7.md).
8. [ADR-0261](../../decisions/search/activation/0261-bound-provider-shadow-selection-to-two-sources-v8.md) imposed a provider-neutral two-source maximum. V8 produced three technically eligible observations but was rejected after mean recall and precision fell to 50%. See [v8](../../evidence/search/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-pilot-v8.md) and the [Codex-Claude measurement report](../../evidence/search/codex-claude-linked-evidence-measurement-report.md).
9. [ADR-0262](../../decisions/search/activation/0262-test-minimal-sufficient-paired-provider-selection-v9.md) removed the cap, added provider-neutral minimal-sufficiency refinement, and bound six reviewed cases to fresh repetition-four paired coordinates with six calls per provider. Passage could authorize only a separately pre-registered 24-by-24 measurement. [V9](../../evidence/search/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-pilot-v9.md) completed all 12 calls without compatibility, usage, replay, privacy, or no-effect failures, but was rejected: Codex precision/recall was 75%/66.67%; Claude was 72.22%/83.33%; both failed 100% per-case recall.

## Ground-truth and claim-coverage pilots v10-v14

10. [ADR-0263](../../decisions/search/activation/0263-require-blind-human-ground-truth-audit-after-v9.md) generated an immutable owner-only packet for two ambiguous cases covering all 29 candidates and 233,735 exact body bytes while omitting providers, selections, expected paths, metrics, and execution coordinates. No eligible reviewer was available. [ADR-0264](../../decisions/search/activation/0264-measure-only-unambiguous-provider-shadow-cases-v10.md) retained the [unreviewed packet](../../evidence/search/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-v9-ground-truth-audit.md), removed it as an activation prerequisite, and excluded both cases without reclassification.
11. [V10](../../evidence/search/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-pilot-v10.md) completed 32/32 paired calls over four unambiguous cases and four repetitions. Claude passed with 100% recall and 89.58% precision. Codex failed with 81.25% recall and 97.92% precision, repeating `terse_release` omission four times and fuzzy-merge omission twice. Claude was 4.6330 times slower and reported 19.85% more tokens. V10 was rejected.
12. [ADR-0265](../../decisions/search/activation/0265-test-claim-coverage-before-minimization-v11.md) changed only the provider-neutral selection protocol after the [v10 diagnosis](../../evidence/search/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-v10-codex-omission-diagnosis.md) localized all six Codex omissions after delivery. [V11](../../evidence/search/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-pilot-v11.md) completed 16/16 paired calls over the two affected cases. Both providers reached 100% recall; Codex precision was 87.50% and Claude precision was 83.34%. It passed only as a diagnostic and authorized a separate four-case confirmation design review.
13. [ADR-0266](../../decisions/search/activation/0266-confirm-claim-coverage-over-four-cases-v12.md) authorized confirmation with the exact v11 protocol. [V12](../../evidence/search/provider-shadow/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-pilot-v12.md) stopped without retry after 17/32 calls when the first hybrid-search Codex turn failed exact-token selection grammar. The first 16 observations retained 100% recall on implementation and terse release; Codex precision was 70.84% and Claude partial precision was 64.59%. V12 was rejected. Another call required bounded privacy-preserving grammar-violation diagnostics.
14. [ADR-0267](../../decisions/search/activation/0267-retain-bounded-selection-violation-codes.md) added a seven-value parser-owned `selectionViolationCodes` field to future unavailable outcomes, attempt journals, observations, and recovery. It retained no raw provider output and changed neither parsing nor acceptance. V12 remains generically classified and immutable; another call required a separately reviewed fresh contract.
15. [ADR-0268](../../decisions/search/activation/0268-diagnose-and-confirm-claim-coverage-v13.md) authorized a fresh diagnostic confirmation with new coordinates and the unchanged v11 protocol. [V13](../../evidence/search/provider-shadow/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-pilot-v13.md) completed 32/32 calls with no unavailable, grammar, replay, privacy, or no-effect failures and 100% recall for both providers. Codex passed at 86.46% mean and 50% minimum precision. Claude reached 75.42% mean precision but failed one implementation-release observation at 40%, so v13 was rejected without retry. Activation remained blocked pending a provider-neutral redundancy diagnosis.
16. The [v13 redundancy diagnosis](../../evidence/search/provider-shadow/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-v13-redundancy-diagnosis.md) found that claim coverage let supplied bodies expand the request into downstream, roadmap, and generic-procedure claims. [ADR-0269](../../decisions/search/activation/0269-test-request-scoped-claim-coverage-v14.md) changed only the provider-neutral selection protocol. [V14](../../evidence/search/provider-shadow/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-pilot-v14.md) consumed exactly 16 calls: Claude passed its provider gates and seven valid Codex observations were exact, but the final Codex carrier included prose and was recorded unavailable. V14 was rejected without retry; activation remained blocked pending a provider-neutral carrier-failure diagnosis.

## Structured-carrier pilots v15-v18

17. The [v14 carrier diagnosis](../../evidence/search/provider-shadow/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-v14-codex-carrier-diagnosis.md) localized failure to prompt-only Codex carrier enforcement: Claude received JSON Schema while the Codex adapter omitted the app-server's available `outputSchema`. [ADR-0270](../../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/0270-enforce-structured-codex-provider-shadow-carriers.md) added opt-in Codex schema forwarding and a common future structured carrier without changing historical replay. It authorized no provider call or activation.
18. [ADR-0271](../../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/0271-test-structured-codex-provider-shadow-carrier-v15.md) authorized one fresh 16-call paired v15 diagnostic on unchanged v14 cases and quality gates. Both providers received the same bounded JSON Schema carrier. A pass could authorize only a separately reviewed four-case confirmation.
19. [V15](../../evidence/search/provider-shadow/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-pilot-v15.md) stopped after one Codex call and zero Claude calls because Codex rejected the redundant `uniqueItems` response-schema keyword before producing an agent message. No-call recovery preserved the unavailable result. [ADR-0272](../../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/0272-remove-unsupported-structured-selection-unique-items.md) removed only that keyword while retaining parser-side duplicate rejection. V15 was rejected; a fresh paired successor required a separate contract.
20. [ADR-0273](../../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/0273-test-compatible-structured-provider-shadow-carrier-v16.md) authorized one fresh 16-call paired v16 diagnostic with new coordinates and the corrected common schema, preserving v14-v15 cases, protocol, ground truth, and quality gates.
21. [V16](../../evidence/search/provider-shadow/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-pilot-v16.md) completed 16/16 calls with valid common structured output, complete usage, and unchanged effects. Claude passed at 100% recall and 79.17% mean precision. Codex reached 100% precision but dropped ADR-0180 while retaining ADR-0227 in one fuzzy-merge repetition, yielding 93.75% mean and 50% minimum recall. V16 was rejected. [ADR-0274](../../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/0274-test-relational-coverage-provider-shadow-v17.md) authorized one fresh 16-call paired diagnostic with a provider-neutral relation-side preservation rule; confirmation and activation remained unauthorized.
22. [V17](../../evidence/search/provider-shadow/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-pilot-v17.md) completed 16/16 calls without retry or technical failure. Both providers retained 100% recall in every observation; Codex achieved 95.83% mean precision and Claude 79.17%. The relational-coverage diagnostic passed. [ADR-0276](../../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/0276-confirm-relational-coverage-provider-shadow-v18.md) authorized one fresh 32-call four-case confirmation; managed context, canary traffic, operational search, and activation remained unauthorized.
23. [V18](../../evidence/search/provider-shadow/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-pilot-v18.md) completed 32/32 fresh calls without retry. Both providers retained 100% recall in every observation; Codex achieved 93.75% mean precision and Claude 76.04%. Confirmation passed and made a separately reviewed managed-context canary and activation design ready. It authorized no context effect, canary traffic, direct-terminal change, operational-search change, or production activation.

## Managed-context canaries

24. [ADR-0277](../../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/0277-stage-managed-context-relational-canary.md) fixed a default-off, managed-run-only canary design with provider-neutral selection, exact E1-E4 evidence separation, fail-closed delivery, and a later 16-run, 32-call isolated read-only ceiling. Implementation and provider-free verification could proceed; live canary execution and context effects required a separate exact prepared checkpoint over an eligible registered corpus.
25. [Managed canary v1](../../evidence/search/provider-shadow/linked-evidence-managed-canary-v1.md) stopped after its first Codex selection call emitted a forbidden command signal; it produced no task sample and remains terminal. [ADR-0280](../../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/0280-add-an-opt-in-codex-responses-no-tool-transport.md) added an optional no-tool Responses transport. [ADR-0281](../../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/0281-prepare-managed-context-canary-v2.md) retained it in an unexecuted 32-call checkpoint. [ADR-0282](../../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/0282-remove-api-key-requirement-from-managed-context-canary.md) rejected a separate API key as a normal product prerequisite and prepared an API-key-free v3 task-only successor. V3 froze exact passed V18 provider-specific selections, revalidated their current projection locally, reused native provider sign-in for 16 ordinary task calls, and made zero selection calls. Preparation and the decision authorized no live traffic.
26. [V3 provider-free preparation](../../evidence/search/provider-shadow/linked-evidence-managed-canary-v3.md) passed all exact evidence bindings with zero calls but retained zero added sources across eight Codex runs and one added source across eight Claude runs. It was rejected before execution because it could not measure comparable linked-context effects. [ADR-0283](../../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/0283-prepare-deterministic-linked-context-canary-v4.md) prepared a provider-neutral successor selecting every bounded current linked candidate in deterministic order. V4 kept native provider sign-in, zero selection calls, and a 16-task-call ceiling while requiring nonempty exact added context in every run.
27. V4 provider-free preparation proved 16/16 nonempty exact selections, with two to six candidates per run, zero provider calls, and a 16-task-call ceiling. Because deterministic runtime implementation followed that snapshot, [ADR-0284](../../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/0284-prepare-exact-deterministic-native-session-canary-v5.md) required a fresh v5 manifest bound to the implemented schema-v2 runtime and built CLI. V5 created no selection adapter, required no user API key, and preserved native signed-in Codex and Claude task sessions. Preparation authorized no live traffic or activation.
28. V5 prepared 16/16 nonempty exact selections and manifest `sha256:85a22b76aa5a07ab58e66eb58035cedbd64b83780b8c356b6cc835f1c90d8f02` with zero calls. Because exact authorization and attempt-before-call execution followed that snapshot, [ADR-0285](../../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/0285-authorize-exact-key-free-managed-canary-v6.md) preserved v5 and required a fresh v6 source/build binding before native-session execution.
29. Managed canaries v6-v10 each stopped at the first newly exposed technical boundary without retry. The sequence corrected exact authorization, read-only role behavior, runtime clock binding, past manifest coordinates, and linked-selection forwarding while preserving immutable failure evidence. V10 completed one Codex model turn but rejected its completion because deterministic linked selections were omitted before E1. [ADR-0290](../../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/0290-forward-linked-selections-for-key-free-managed-canary-v11.md) fixed only that forwarding boundary and prepared a fresh v11.
30. [Managed canary v11](../../evidence/search/provider-shadow/linked-evidence-managed-canary-v11.md) completed all eight Codex and eight Claude native-session runs with zero technical failures, selection calls, retries, API keys, or repository mutations. Both providers retained 100% recall. Codex passed at 81.25% mean precision; Claude reached 50.14%, with three runs below 50%. Deterministic selection removed all 16 selection calls and halved the two-stage provider-call ceiling, but activation remained blocked.
31. [ADR-0291](../../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/0291-constrain-provider-neutral-e4-references-for-managed-canary-v12.md) authorized a provider-neutral prompt-only E4 constraint and one fresh paired confirmation. [Managed canary v12](../../evidence/search/provider-shadow/linked-evidence-managed-canary-v12.md) completed 16/16 runs without technical failure. Claude precision improved to 65.97% but retained two per-run failures; Codex precision improved to 91.67%, while two recall failures reduced mean recall to 87.5%. [ADR-0292](../../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/0292-reject-prompt-only-e4-constraint-after-managed-canary-v12.md) rejected and rolled back the instruction. A successor required a typed task-evidence versus governance-context boundary; activation remained default-off.
32. [ADR-0293](../../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/0293-type-task-evidence-and-governance-context.md) added required `task_evidence` and `governance_context` roles from context planning through E3 receipts. Codex and Claude received the same role metadata, governance documents received no E4 token, and task evidence retained exact tokens. Common tests preserved raw unfiltered E4 observation and rejected role substitution. No provider call or activation was authorized.
33. [ADR-0294](../../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/0294-prepare-typed-context-managed-canary-v13.md) fixed a provider-free v13 checkpoint over the exact v12 cases, repetitions, ordering, and accuracy gates. It bound the typed-boundary source bytes, v12 terminal evidence, repository snapshot, built CLI, rollback predecessor, and an eight-Codex plus eight-Claude native-session ceiling. The v13 entrypoint exposed preparation and status only; zero calls and no activation were authorized.
34. [ADR-0295](../../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/typed-context/0295-authorize-typed-context-managed-canary-v14.md) translated the user's explicit execution direction into a fresh source-current v14 checkpoint because v13 remained immutable and preparation-only. V14 bound exact authorization and fail-first execution sources, persisted attempts before calls, revalidated source and build before every sequential task, and preserved the eight-Codex plus eight-Claude ceiling with zero selection calls, API keys, retries, or activation.
35. [Managed canary v14](../../evidence/search/provider-shadow/linked-evidence-managed-canary-v14.md) completed all 16 native-session runs with zero technical failure, selection calls, retries, API keys, writes, or repository mutations. Both providers achieved 100% recall. Codex passed at 87.5% mean precision; Claude reached 60.07%, with two runs below 50%. Mean duration improved 7.61% for Codex and 12.95% for Claude relative to v12, and deterministic selection retained the 50% provider-call reduction. [ADR-0296](../../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/typed-context/0296-reject-activation-after-typed-context-managed-canary-v14.md) rejected activation and required a task-evidence eligibility diagnosis before another call.
36. [ADR-0297](../../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/typed-context/0297-separate-claim-reference-eligibility-from-task-evidence.md) separated direct claim authority from linked supporting context. Linked-only evidence remained E1-selected, E2-loaded, and E3-injected but received no E4 token; direct task provenance remained eligible and won when both occurred.
37. V15 stopped after one Codex task call when measurement read a missing bounded outcome field. [ADR-0298](../../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/typed-context/0298-stop-v15-on-measurement-projection-failure.md) preserved the failure and derived sorted unique reference IDs from canonical E4 into the bounded summary.
38. V16 stopped after 11 accepted observations and a twelfth completed task call reached the 64-record canary limit. [ADR-0299](../../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/typed-context/0299-stop-v16-and-expand-canary-observation-capacity.md) preserved the terminal population and raised the bounded owner-only record cap to 128 without deleting history.
39. V17 stopped after 10 accepted observations when the next pre-provider preparation found 4,105 relevant execution events above the 4,096 cap. [ADR-0300](../../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/typed-context/0300-stop-v17-and-expand-complete-event-capacity.md) preserved the terminal population and raised the complete-event cap to 8,192 while retaining the 16 MiB cap and full privacy validation.
40. [Managed canary v18](../../evidence/search/provider-shadow/linked-evidence-managed-canary-v18.md) completed all 16 fresh runs with zero technical failures, selection calls, retries, API keys, or repository mutations. Both providers achieved 100% recall; Codex achieved 83.33% mean precision and Claude 79.17%, with no per-run failure. [ADR-0301](../../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/typed-context/0301-activate-deterministic-claim-eligible-linked-context.md) activated the zero-selection-call deterministic path for ordinary managed runs and kept the exact canary separately authorized.
41. The [paired lexical-only comparison](../../evidence/search/paired-uplift/lexical-linked-uplift-v1.md) completed 32 Codex and Claude runs. Linked context reduced equal-provider claim recall and complete-answer rate by 6.25 percentage points each. [ADR-0302](../../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/typed-context/0302-retire-linked-default-and-use-search-engine-ports.md) retired ordinary linked selection, preserved the exact canary as default-off history, and routed future retrieval improvement through the existing search-engine ports.
