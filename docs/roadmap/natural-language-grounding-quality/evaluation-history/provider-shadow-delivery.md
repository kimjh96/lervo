# Provider-shadow and managed-canary delivery

This history records provider-shadow authorization, failures, diagnostics, and confirmation. V18 passed confirmation and made a separately reviewed managed-context canary and activation design ready; it authorized no context effect, canary traffic, direct-terminal change, operational-search change, or production activation.

<a id="provider-shadow-pilots-v1-v9"></a>

## Provider-shadow pilots v1-v9

1. [ADR-0253](../../../decisions/search/activation/0253-pre-register-effect-free-provider-shadow-pilot.md) authorized 24 calls. [V1](../../../evidence/search/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-pilot-v1.md) reproduced the 24-sample, 16-candidate, and 130,302-byte bounds, then stopped after one call. Delivery mapped `sourceId` to `documentId`, but parsing read `documentId` from original records and rejected every token as unknown. No canonical observation persisted. A corrected post-stop replay selected exactly two reviewed relevant sources with 100% recall and precision, but lacked shadow-run, local-session, target-turn, adapter-receipt, and duration coordinates and could not replace the immutable record. Primary-effect digests were unchanged.
2. A successor required a reviewed contract and authority, preservation of stopped v1, one candidate identity shape, bounded unavailable records for every completed rejected turn, refusal after failure, and a fresh population without retries or borrowed results. Passage could authorize only a separate canary-design decision. [ADR-0254](../../../decisions/search/activation/0254-pre-register-recoverable-provider-shadow-pilot-v2.md) was superseded before execution when Claude measurement became required; ADR-0255 replaced it.
3. [ADR-0255](../../../decisions/search/activation/0255-authorize-paired-codex-claude-provider-shadow-v2.md) prepared 24 pairs and stopped after one valid Codex and one unavailable Claude observation. Codex selected both reviewed sources with 100% precision and recall in 11.438 seconds, reducing selected-body bytes by 90.28%. Claude completed but failed the aggregate-result boundary; the cause was not retained. See [v2](../../../evidence/search/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-pilot-v2.md).
4. [ADR-0256](../../../decisions/search/activation/0256-correct-claude-provider-shadow-observability-v3.md) removed Claude tools and separated bounded result failures. The first Claude turn lacked provider-neutral usage because the adapter discarded SDK `modelUsage`; no Codex call occurred. See [v3](../../../evidence/search/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-pilot-v3.md). [ADR-0257](../../../decisions/search/activation/0257-observe-claude-sdk-usage-provider-shadow-v4.md) added tested cumulative-usage normalization with reviewed `patch` intent. V4 passed usage but failed strict E4-only grammar. Across v2-v4, four calls occurred; fail-closed stopping avoided 47 of 51. No valid cross-provider speed, accuracy, token, or cost comparison exists. See the [final report](../../../evidence/search/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-pilot-v4.md).
5. [ADR-0258](../../../decisions/search/activation/0258-correct-claude-structured-selection-provider-shadow-v5.md) retained legacy grammar, added opt-in Claude SDK JSON Schema output, and authorized three fail-first calls. V5 stopped after one turn because the unavailable validator rejected the new coordinate prefix. No exact outcome survived; v5 is immutable and evidence-ineligible. See [v5](../../../evidence/search/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-pilot-v5.md).
6. [ADR-0259](../../../decisions/search/activation/0259-preserve-claude-selection-failure-provider-shadow-v6.md) persisted bounded outcomes before observation construction and authorized three fresh calls. V6 stopped after classifying its first turn `provider_shadow_tool_activity`: the SDK used an end-turn tool carrier for JSON Schema although no ordinary Claude tools were exposed. See [v6](../../../evidence/search/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-pilot-v6.md).
7. [ADR-0260](../../../decisions/search/activation/0260-classify-claude-structured-output-framing-provider-shadow-v7.md) permitted only one provider-native output-framing event; commands and other tools remained fail-closed. V7 produced three eligible Claude observations with 100% recall and complete measurements, but redundancy reduced precision to 52.22%, below the 65% gate. See [v7](../../../evidence/search/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-pilot-v7.md).
8. [ADR-0261](../../../decisions/search/activation/0261-bound-provider-shadow-selection-to-two-sources-v8.md) imposed a provider-neutral two-source maximum. V8 produced three technically eligible observations but was rejected after mean recall and precision fell to 50%. See [v8](../../../evidence/search/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-pilot-v8.md) and the [Codex-Claude measurement report](../../../evidence/search/codex-claude-linked-evidence-measurement-report.md).
9. [ADR-0262](../../../decisions/search/activation/0262-test-minimal-sufficient-paired-provider-selection-v9.md) removed the cap, added provider-neutral minimal-sufficiency refinement, and bound six reviewed cases to fresh repetition-four paired coordinates with six calls per provider. Passage could authorize only a separately pre-registered 24-by-24 measurement. [V9](../../../evidence/search/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-pilot-v9.md) completed all 12 calls without compatibility, usage, replay, privacy, or no-effect failures, but was rejected: Codex precision/recall was 75%/66.67%; Claude was 72.22%/83.33%. Both failed 100% per-case recall.

<a id="ground-truth-and-claim-coverage-pilots-v10-v14"></a>

## Ground-truth and claim-coverage pilots v10-v14

10. [ADR-0263](../../../decisions/search/activation/0263-require-blind-human-ground-truth-audit-after-v9.md) generated an immutable owner-only packet for two ambiguous cases covering all 29 candidates and 233,735 exact body bytes. It omitted providers, selections, expected paths, metrics, and execution coordinates. No eligible reviewer was available. [ADR-0264](../../../decisions/search/activation/0264-measure-only-unambiguous-provider-shadow-cases-v10.md) retained the [unreviewed packet](../../../evidence/search/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-v9-ground-truth-audit.md), removed it as an activation prerequisite, and excluded both cases without reclassification.
11. [V10](../../../evidence/search/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-pilot-v10.md) completed 32/32 paired calls over four unambiguous cases and four repetitions. Claude passed with 100% recall and 89.58% precision. Codex failed with 81.25% recall and 97.92% precision, repeating `terse_release` omission four times and fuzzy-merge omission twice. Claude was 4.6330 times slower and reported 19.85% more tokens. V10 was rejected.
12. [ADR-0265](../../../decisions/search/activation/0265-test-claim-coverage-before-minimization-v11.md) changed only the provider-neutral selection protocol after the [v10 diagnosis](../../../evidence/search/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-v10-codex-omission-diagnosis.md) localized all six Codex omissions after delivery. [V11](../../../evidence/search/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-pilot-v11.md) completed 16/16 paired calls over the two affected cases. Both providers reached 100% recall; Codex precision was 87.50% and Claude precision was 83.34%. It passed only as a diagnostic and authorized a separate four-case confirmation design review.
13. [ADR-0266](../../../decisions/search/activation/0266-confirm-claim-coverage-over-four-cases-v12.md) authorized confirmation with the exact v11 protocol. [V12](../../../evidence/search/provider-shadow/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-pilot-v12.md) stopped without retry after 17/32 calls when the first hybrid-search Codex turn failed exact-token selection grammar. The first 16 observations retained 100% recall on implementation and terse release; Codex precision was 70.84% and Claude partial precision was 64.59%. V12 was rejected. Another call required bounded privacy-preserving grammar-violation diagnostics.
14. [ADR-0267](../../../decisions/search/activation/0267-retain-bounded-selection-violation-codes.md) added a seven-value parser-owned `selectionViolationCodes` field to future unavailable outcomes, attempt journals, observations, and recovery. It retained no raw provider output and changed neither parsing nor acceptance. V12 remains generically classified and immutable; another call required a separately reviewed fresh contract.
15. [ADR-0268](../../../decisions/search/activation/0268-diagnose-and-confirm-claim-coverage-v13.md) authorized fresh diagnostic confirmation with new coordinates and the unchanged v11 protocol. [V13](../../../evidence/search/provider-shadow/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-pilot-v13.md) completed 32/32 calls with no unavailable, grammar, replay, privacy, or no-effect failures and 100% recall for both providers. Codex passed at 86.46% mean and 50% minimum precision. Claude reached 75.42% mean precision but failed one implementation-release observation at 40%. V13 was rejected without retry; activation remained blocked pending a provider-neutral redundancy diagnosis.
16. The [v13 redundancy diagnosis](../../../evidence/search/provider-shadow/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-v13-redundancy-diagnosis.md) found that claim coverage let supplied bodies expand the request into downstream, roadmap, and generic-procedure claims. [ADR-0269](../../../decisions/search/activation/0269-test-request-scoped-claim-coverage-v14.md) changed only the provider-neutral selection protocol. [V14](../../../evidence/search/provider-shadow/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-pilot-v14.md) consumed exactly 16 calls: Claude passed its provider gates and seven valid Codex observations were exact, but the final Codex carrier included prose and was recorded unavailable. V14 was rejected without retry; activation remained blocked pending a provider-neutral carrier-failure diagnosis.

<a id="structured-carrier-pilots-v15-v18"></a>

## Structured-carrier pilots v15-v18

17. The [v14 carrier diagnosis](../../../evidence/search/provider-shadow/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-v14-codex-carrier-diagnosis.md) localized failure to prompt-only Codex carrier enforcement: Claude received JSON Schema while the Codex adapter omitted the app-server's available `outputSchema`. [ADR-0270](../../../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/0270-enforce-structured-codex-provider-shadow-carriers.md) added opt-in Codex schema forwarding and a common future structured carrier without changing historical replay. It authorized no provider call or activation.
18. [ADR-0271](../../../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/0271-test-structured-codex-provider-shadow-carrier-v15.md) authorized one fresh 16-call paired v15 diagnostic on unchanged v14 cases and quality gates. Both providers received the same bounded JSON Schema carrier. A pass could authorize only a separately reviewed four-case confirmation.
19. [V15](../../../evidence/search/provider-shadow/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-pilot-v15.md) stopped after one Codex call and zero Claude calls because Codex rejected the redundant `uniqueItems` response-schema keyword before producing an agent message. No-call recovery preserved the unavailable result. [ADR-0272](../../../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/0272-remove-unsupported-structured-selection-unique-items.md) removed only that keyword, retaining parser-side duplicate rejection. V15 was rejected; a fresh paired successor required a separate contract.
20. [ADR-0273](../../../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/0273-test-compatible-structured-provider-shadow-carrier-v16.md) authorized one fresh 16-call paired v16 diagnostic with new coordinates and the corrected common schema, preserving v14-v15 cases, protocol, ground truth, and quality gates.
21. [V16](../../../evidence/search/provider-shadow/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-pilot-v16.md) completed 16/16 calls with valid common structured output, complete usage, and unchanged effects. Claude passed at 100% recall and 79.17% mean precision. Codex reached 100% precision but dropped ADR-0180 while retaining ADR-0227 in one fuzzy-merge repetition, yielding 93.75% mean and 50% minimum recall. V16 was rejected. [ADR-0274](../../../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/0274-test-relational-coverage-provider-shadow-v17.md) authorized one fresh 16-call paired diagnostic with a provider-neutral relation-side preservation rule; confirmation and activation remained unauthorized.
22. [V17](../../../evidence/search/provider-shadow/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-pilot-v17.md) completed 16/16 calls without retry or technical failure. Both providers retained 100% recall in every observation; Codex achieved 95.83% mean precision and Claude 79.17%. The relational-coverage diagnostic passed. [ADR-0276](../../../decisions/search/activation/provider-shadow-structured/0276-confirm-relational-coverage-provider-shadow-v18.md) authorized one fresh 32-call four-case confirmation; managed context, canary traffic, operational search, and activation remained unauthorized.
23. [V18](../../../evidence/search/provider-shadow/linked-evidence-provider-shadow-pilot-v18.md) completed 32/32 fresh calls without retry. Both providers retained 100% recall in every observation; Codex achieved 93.75% mean precision and Claude 76.04%. Confirmation passed and made a separately reviewed managed-context canary and activation design ready. It authorized no context effect, canary traffic, direct-terminal change, operational-search change, or production activation.
