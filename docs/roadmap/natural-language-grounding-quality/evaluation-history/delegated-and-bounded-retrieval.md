# Delegated and bounded retrieval

A same-model [delegated pilot](../../evidence/search/delegated-retrieval-pilot-v1.md) compared direct read-only exploration with top-three lexical assistance across eight cases. Direct exploration found every expected path in 7/8 cases versus 5/8, using 38 repository commands and 1,203,427 observed tokens versus 10 commands and 592,630 tokens. It recovered all three Korean-only no-hit probes. Lexical assistance was faster when recommendations existed but, despite high provider confidence, missed secondary sources in two multi-source cases. ADR-0226 retired `local-hybrid-v1`, retained `lexical-v1`, and directed evaluation toward bounded no-hit and mandatory-verification fallback.

[Bounded fallback v1](../../evidence/search/bounded-retrieval-fallback-v1.md) was rejected. Across 16 observations per arm, direct passed 12, lexical-assisted passed 13, and bounded fallback passed 11. Fallback used 93.33% of direct tokens, took 105.11% of direct duration, and passed both repetitions in five cases against a minimum of six. Mandatory verification lost a lexically recommended Node-fleet source twice and completed fuzzy-merge evidence once. V1 has no activation authority.
