# Assisted retrieval iterations

- [`lexical-assisted-v2`](../../evidence/search/lexical-assisted-v2.md) was rejected. Direct passed 15/24, permissive v1 13/24, and v2 10/24. V2 used 34.71% of direct tokens and 54.26% of its duration; p50 and p95 fell 48.58% and 58.84%. Recall fell 18.75 points; only three cases passed every repetition; one relevant recommendation was dropped; and no-hit p95 and deviation worsened 16.06% and 91.21%. Provider-named gaps did not reliably detect incomplete lexical bundles.
- [`catalog-assisted-v3`](../../evidence/search/catalog-assisted-v3.md) exposed all sanitized corpus paths and first headings in a deterministic 37,778-byte directory while retaining the top three lexical bodies. It passed 11/24 versus 15/24 direct, passed every repetition for three cases versus four, and reached 75.00% recall versus 77.08%. It used 46.57% of direct tokens and 60.75% of its duration; no-hit p95 and deviation improved 21.03% and 7.82%. It always missed Node architecture, cross-provider exact merge, and the execution specification, and had one catalog-contract failure.
- [`core-term-assisted-v4`](../../evidence/search/core-term-assisted-v4.md) used the same canonical core for every query, added the top lexical body for each bounded ASCII query term, and retained the v3 catalog only for no-hits. It passed 16/24 versus 15/24 direct and improved recall from 70.83% to 81.25%, but only four cases passed every repetition. Two provider-contract failures, three coverage-contract failures, incomplete token observations, and two omitted injected relevant sources caused rejection. The fixed core repaired Node architecture and the execution specification; exact delivery did not ensure repeatable reference.
- [`reference-marker-assisted-v5`](../../evidence/search/reference-marker-assisted-v5.md) retained v4 coverage and required one-to-one supplied-source paths and exact output evidence markers. It passed 9/24 grounded-answer verdicts versus 14/24 direct, with only two cases passing every repetition. Five invalid structured results failed provider, coverage, and reference contracts; four relevant coverage sources were dropped; and token observations were incomplete. Its 19 valid results produced 44 exact references without marker mismatch or unreferenced relevant returned sources. Markers prove reference, never understanding or internal use.
- [`marker-derived-paths-v6`](../../evidence/search/marker-derived-paths-v6.md) corrected fuzzy ground truth to ADR-0180 plus ADR-0227, derived paths only from exact output markers, and separated bounded-prose failures from retrieval and provider execution. The corrected case passed 3/3 in both arms. All 24 turns had complete usage, 51 exact references, and no marker or provider-execution failures. V6 passed 13/24 versus 19/24 direct, passed every repetition for three cases versus six, and reached 64.58% recall versus 87.50%. It failed three no-hit coverage and response contracts, dropped one relevant recommendation and two relevant coverage sources, and had 5.14% worse no-hit latency deviation. It used 46.42% of direct tokens and 48.26% of its duration.
- [`staged-evidence-selection-v7`](../../evidence/search/staged-evidence-v7.md) separated exact token-only selection from an answer turn receiving only selected bodies. All 24 selection contracts and six no-hit selection-load-reference chains passed. V7 passed 16/24 versus 17/24 direct, reached 79.17% recall versus 83.33%, omitted two selected and loaded execution-specification references, and produced one forbidden source marker. With complete usage and no provider failures, it used 40.79% of direct tokens and 53.48% of its duration and improved both no-hit latency criteria.
- [`linked-evidence-reconciliation-v8`](../../evidence/search/linked-evidence-reconciliation-v8.md) added a deterministic one-hop inline Markdown-link prefix from lexical recommendations, bounded to eight linked bodies and 65,536 bytes, and required the final exact reference set to equal the selected-and-loaded set. It passed 21/24 versus 16/24 direct, passed every repetition for seven cases versus four, and reached 100% recall without provider, selection, coverage, reconciliation, marker, response, or preservation failures. One direct-control provider failure left control usage incomplete and failed the token-ratio criterion. V8 was rejected but warranted exact replication.
- Unchanged [`v9`](../../evidence/search/linked-evidence-reconciliation-confirmation-v9.md) passed every pre-registered criterion: 21/24 versus 17/24 direct, every repetition for seven cases versus five, and 100% recall versus 83.33%. Usage was complete with no provider failures. It used 54.10% of direct tokens and 57.93% of its duration, passed both no-hit latency criteria, and passed all candidate-evidence and preservation contracts. Three failures repeated terse-release ADR-0223 overselection without missing an expected path. This confirmed the candidate without activating it.

Operational search remained `lexical-v1`; no rejected iteration was an activation candidate. [ADR-0248](../../decisions/search/0248-stage-linked-evidence-reconciliation-activation.md) classified linked-evidence reconciliation as post-recommendation context selection, kept direct terminals unchanged, and authorized only an effect-free managed-run Phase A deterministic local shadow projection with owner-only observations. Extra provider turns, changed E1-E4 context, and canary traffic required later reviewed decisions.
