# Paperclip and Lervo comparison research

Status: `accepted input`  
As of: 2026-08-30

## Objective

Compare Lervo with Paperclip to identify capability gaps, adaptable ideas, Lervo strengths, and unsafe copying boundaries.

Paperclip is the more complete agent-organization operating product; Lervo is the stricter repository execution and evidence kernel. They are not direct substitutes. Prefer a thin operating projection over Lervo's workforce, workstream, evidence, and deliberation contracts, not a Paperclip-style database authority inside Lervo.

## Resume from here

1. Build a deterministic, read-only workforce and workstream board.
2. Add interaction threads, mention resolution, and inbox routing.
3. Add observed usage and cost reporting without hard stops.
4. Prototype finding blockers, pending approvals, and cost spikes.
5. Continue with bounded collaboration, budget, routine, lineage, and capability views.
