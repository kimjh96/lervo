Status: `historical negative and development evidence`  
Observed: 2026-08-31

## Purpose

This bounded history preserves the iterations that produced the frozen v12 confirmation. None independently authorizes activation. Every revision retained prior cases and gates; each change addressed a measured general failure rather than an expected-path alias or provider-specific candidate advantage.

V3 was a provider-free preparation of the v4 workspace correction and made no eligible live claim. The v10 evaluator defect led directly to symmetric contract, tool, command, and provider-error gates in v11. V11 remained development evidence because all eight cases had been observed during iteration; it justified a fresh holdout confirmation, not activation.

## Rejected passage controls

A build-time passage index raised build p50 to about 3.5 times lexical-v1 and caused a Korean success regression. Query-time MaxP restored build cost but raised warm-query p50 to about 3.4-4.3 times baseline. Both were removed before the final candidate.
