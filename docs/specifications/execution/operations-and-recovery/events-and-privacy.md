# Events and Privacy

The execution events and privacy specification defines allowlisted payloads, workforce events, knowledge and terminal integration events, additive terminal-observation compatibility, workstream snapshots, activation receipts, coverage, privacy exclusions, and failure meaning. It owns reader compatibility, writer activation, and failure codes.

Events remain canonical hash-chained evidence. Absent telemetry must not imply success or provider use. Stored managed linked-evidence canary records keep the bounds and privacy exclusions of its historical specification; [ADR-0528](../../../decisions/search/activation/0528-remove-the-linked-evidence-canary-and-shadow.md) removed their writer.

Stage observation and performance defines timing, environment, projection, and performance gates. Missing evidence must not imply success, zero usage, duration, or ancestry.
