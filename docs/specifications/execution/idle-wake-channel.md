# Idle wakeups and the event-store wake channel

Status: `active`
As of: 2026-11-20

Defines how each failure of the wake path is prevented, recovered or made visible, and the test that proves it. [ADR-0602](../../decisions/knowledge/maintenance/0602-wait-on-change-tokens-and-guard-idle-timers.md) retains the decision and its rationale.

## Failure modes

Each failure is prevented, recovered or visible; the test that proves it follows the arrow.

- **A writer exits right after committing.** Prevented: the ping is written synchronously before `transact` returns. → `change-channel.test.ts` (a child pings and calls `process.exit(0)`; a child commits through a real `SQLiteEventStore`).
- **A rolled-back transaction.** Prevented: only a commit pings. → `event-store-change-ping.test.ts` counts the FIFO bytes.
- **A stopped, slow or full subscriber.** Prevented and visible: writes are non-blocking, EAGAIN means pings wait, others are unaffected, and the stall shows within 30 seconds from the commit-time ring, which racing writers fill under the write lock. → `change-channel.test.ts` (a `SIGSTOP`ped subscriber; an unobserved commit; two racing writer processes).
- **A crashed subscriber's FIFO.** Recovered: with no reader on this kernel the open fails with ENXIO, and the writer removes the FIFO, its records and its temporary files through a tombstone. → `change-channel.test.ts` (a `SIGKILL`ed child).
- **Another host, a previous boot, a clock step, a host-name change or an unknown identity.** Recovered or visible: removal needs ENXIO or a free lock from this host's key; a live FIFO with a stepped boot key stays; another key is reported, kept and still pinged. → `change-channel.test.ts`, `process-identity.test.ts`.
- **A commit that races the subscription.** Prevented: the sources are armed before the first token read. → `change-token-watch.test.ts`.
- **Coalesced pings.** Prevented: a ping carries no data, each wake reads the token, and the caller reads from its own cursor. → `change-token-watch.test.ts`.
- **A failed or removed FIFO, or a removed run directory.** Recovered and visible: every check rebuilds the channel and catches up. While it is missing, the subscriber's record raises `event_channel_unavailable`. → `change-channel.test.ts`, `change-token-watch.test.ts`.
- **A writer that does not ping (an older binary).** Recovered and visible: the directory watch wakes the watch and raises `event_channel_writer_without_ping`. → `change-token-watch.test.ts`.
- **No directory watch, or no source at all.** Visible: `event_channel_detector_unavailable` and the watch's health. → `change-token-watch.test.ts`.
- **A symlinked, foreign-owned or group-writable run directory.** Prevented and visible: writers never write there, and `event_channel_refused` is raised. → `change-channel.test.ts`, `wake-channel-attention.test.ts`.
- **A filesystem without FIFOs, or no `mkfifo`.** Visible: `event_channel_unsupported` or `event_channel_mkfifo_missing` in that subscriber's record. → `change-channel.test.ts`.
- **`lervo state` status, reset and prune.** Prevented: the FIFOs are outside derived state, and channel and watch records are lifecycle-owned. → `state-plans-event-channel.test.ts` (with a live subscriber).
- **A writer that cannot open a FIFO (another uid or mode).** Visible: `event_channel_subscriber_unreachable`, cleared when that writer's pings all land. → `change-channel.test.ts`.
- **A repository watch that cannot be armed (ENOSPC, EMFILE).** Visible: `repository_watch_unavailable` with the fix, in that watch's own record, cleared when it is armed. → `repository-watch-state.test.ts`, `wake-channel-attention.test.ts`.
- **History rewritten after a reader verified it.** Prevented at use: the store rejects a row whose hash or neighbour links changed wherever it is read, readers act only on verified copies, and publication and Hub batches re-prove their exact range. → `event-store.test.ts`, `workstream-revision-reader.test.ts`, `delivery-verification.test.ts`, `worker.test.ts`, `workstream-replication.test.ts`.
- **Windows.** Prevented: the same guarantees over ping files and exclusive locks, with no pipe to impersonate a writer. A watch that cannot be armed raises `event_channel_unavailable`. → `change-channel.test.ts` in the Windows job.
- **An idle host.** Prevented: there is no token read, store call, database open or timer. → `resident-idle-wait.test.ts` (12 simulated hours).

## Deadlines and push (step 2)

Nothing runs on a cadence. A timer is armed only for a known moment (a due time, a lease expiry, a failure retry), and Hub state arrives as a push. A wall-clock step or a sleep only ever makes a deadline late, never early: timers run on the monotonic clock and fire only names whose wall-clock moment has passed.

- **A resident with nothing due.** Prevented: no timer at all, and no age bound on an unchanged fingerprint. → `loop.test.ts` (no `setTimeout` or `setInterval` while asleep), `loop-health.test.ts`, `resident-cycle.test.ts`, `resident-deadline-timer.test.ts`.
- **Hub state with no local signal** (owner-rule candidates, host records, definitions, the lease holder). Recovered at once: each Hub write notifies the `knowledge` topic of the repository's other hosts, never the writer's own; the resident holds that wait open and folds its token into the fingerprint, so the next cycle runs in full. → `knowledge-change-wait-http.test.ts`, `knowledge-convergence-hosts-http.test.ts`, `hub-knowledge-changes.test.ts`.
- **A held wait whose connection died where no keepalive sees it** (a proxy hop, such as Tailscale serve in front of a forwarded Hub). Recovered within the decided end-to-end bound: the Hub answers every held wait within ten minutes, which also reclaims its own waiter, the client waits again at once without running a cycle, and a request unanswered a minute past the bound is ended and reconnected. TCP keepalive (15 seconds) still ends a dead first hop at once. → `runtime-change-notifications.test.ts`, `hub-held-request.test.ts`, `runtime-idle-wait.test.ts`.
- **A Hub wait that fails.** Recovered and visible: it retries with exponential backoff, and a failure that repeats raises `hub_knowledge_changes_unavailable` or `hub_runtime_changes_unavailable` until it is answered again. → `hub-knowledge-changes.test.ts`, `runtime-idle-wait.test.ts`.
- **A Hub older than this release** (HTTP 400 or 404 on `hold`, `hostHash` or `topic=knowledge`). Visible and safe: both waits raise `hub_upgrade_required`, whose remedy is to deploy the Hub first. Workstream and command waits fall back to its bounded legacy hold and ask for the open hold again from a minute after the refusal, doubling to an hour. The knowledge wait runs one full cycle an hour, the cadence before this release, and asks the Hub again from a minute, doubling to ten minutes, so an upgraded Hub is used within minutes. → `runtime-idle-wait.test.ts`, `hub-knowledge-changes.test.ts`.
- **Startup that stopped waiting for the first Hub answer.** Recovered: an answer that arrives after the ten-second bound, or after a failed first wait released startup, counts as a change, so the cycle that ran without it runs again. → `hub-knowledge-changes.test.ts`.
- **Two hosts waking each other with their own records.** Prevented: the Hub compares records without any observation time, at any depth, and a host never publishes a record whose facts the Hub already holds, ignoring the presence a listing adds. An earlier Hub, whose listing has no presence, judges peers by record age, so there a host publishes every turn, the cadence before this release. → `delegation-single-writer.test.ts` (two hosts with a changing nested observation time settle), `knowledge-convergence-host-record.test.ts`, `knowledge-convergence-hosts-http.test.ts`.
- **A peer's host record or presence change waking every host's full cycle.** Prevented: each `knowledge` answer names the kinds changed since that host's last answer (`lease`, `candidate`, `definition`, `host`, `presence`). The lease holder's own arrival or departure is a `lease` change too. Only an answer whose every kind is `host` or `presence` leaves the cycle token alone and runs a forced continuity turn; any other kind, including one a later Hub adds, and an answer without kinds (a reconnection, a Hub restart) run a cycle. → `runtime-change-notifications.test.ts`, `knowledge-change-wait-http.test.ts`, `hub-knowledge-changes.test.ts`.
- **An answer lost on the way.** Never hides a change: the Hub keeps the kinds changed since each of the last few cursors it handed a host, adding every change to each, so a host that asks again with its earlier cursor is told everything since. A cursor it has no record for is told no kinds, which runs a cycle. A scope keeps cursors for at most 256 hosts; past that, the host that waited least recently and is not waiting is forgotten, and told no kinds when it returns. → `runtime-change-notifications.test.ts` (a lost lease answer, a later presence change, then the old cursor; the host cap).
- **A host of an earlier release, which names no host when it waits.** Never read as gone: the Hub reports no presence for a host it has not seen waiting, and peers judge it by its record's age. → `runtime-change-notifications.test.ts`, `knowledge-convergence-hosts-http.test.ts`.
- **A lease released by its holder.** Recovered at once: the release notifies the waiting host, which takes over without waiting for the expiry; on the same checkout a release command rewrites the watched owner fence. A holder that vanishes without releasing ends its Hub wait; the Hub pushes that as a lease change, so a waiting host looks again, sees the holder absent and takes over at its lease expiry, the one deadline. → `non-owner-takeover.test.ts` (no clock skew, with the whole lease and with twenty seconds left, and a holder that vanishes).
- **Peer liveness.** Prevented from needing a heartbeat: the Hub reports whether each host holds its `knowledge` wait, and a peer is unreported only after ten minutes without one; a disconnection is pushed after a five-second grace. → `runtime-change-notifications.test.ts`, `continuity-findings.test.ts`.
- **Continuity behind a busy worker lock.** Prevented from spinning: a busy lock is its own result. When this resident's cycle holds it, continuity sets no deadline and turns again once the cycle ends; another process's lock is retried with backoff from the retry bound. A due renewal this host may not write is not polled for: taking over ownership runs a forced continuity turn at once, and the owner's renewed record reaches a follower as a Hub push. → `resident-continuity-turns.test.ts`, `delegation-single-writer.test.ts`, `loop-owner-deadline.test.ts`.
- **Continuity.** Recovered on change: it reconciles when its host-local inputs differ from the ones it recorded, when a Hub change forces it, or at its own next time-based moment; its policy, owner-host and provider-pause files are watched. Its state is stale only once that moment passed unobserved. → `continuity-findings.test.ts`, `convergence-continuity.test.ts`.
- **A projection deadline during a cycle.** Prevented from spinning: an age-only offer's deadline is the age bound, a due retry refreshes, and the timer refuses a deadline set back to the past moment it just fired at. → `loop-health.test.ts` (a moved head during a running cycle), `resident-projection-fence.test.ts`, `resident-deadline-timer.test.ts`.
- **A hold released during a cycle.** Recovered: the cycle runs once more. → `loop-health.test.ts`.
- **A watched directory created later, removed and created again, or a watch that failed, or a change during a CLI host scan.** Recovered: the nearest existing ancestor is watched and re-armed toward it, a removed directory or a failed watch is re-armed, and the CLI host keeps its watch across scans. → `hub-knowledge-changes.test.ts`, `knowledge-host-residents.test.ts`.
- **An edit to `.git/info/exclude`.** Recovered at once: `.git/info` is watched. → `repository-change-watch.test.ts`.
- **A failure that keeps recurring** (a change loop cycle or its deadline read, an unreadable writer coordinate, a repository watch that cannot be armed or keeps failing right after it armed, a lost or flapping replica source). Recovered and visible: exponential backoff to a ceiling, reset only by a source that stayed up as long as its delay, and reported until it clears. → `conversation-change-loop.test.ts`, `knowledge-worker-admission.test.ts`, `knowledge-worker-wakeups.test.ts`, `repository-replica-watch.test.ts`.
- **A change loop with no change signal** (no change source in its configuration, or one that cannot be armed). Never silent: it runs again at least every minute, honouring a sooner deadline, and raises `conversation_change_signal_unavailable` on the repository until it stops. In a Node every loop has a source: the event store watch, the conversation queue watch, the supervision sources, and committed discussion writes for discussion deadlines. → `conversation-change-loop.test.ts`, `conversation-repository-signal-loop.test.ts`, `conversation-interaction-retry-loop.test.ts`, `conversation-discussion-runtime.test.ts`.
- **A foreground write the resident reads but no event records** (an owner turn queued for capture, a stored owner-rule candidate, a freshness decision, a dismissed capture, or a publication run by `lervo knowledge convergence owner publish` that asks for a retry, such as a pull request still waiting on checks, which only the resident's own publish would otherwise arm; the resident's look then takes one step of the wait's schedule). Recovered at once: the command writes `.lervo/state/knowledge-resident-request.json`, which the resident's repository watch reacts to; the resident's own writes never do, so it never wakes itself (ADR-0606). → `repository-change-watch.test.ts`, `knowledge-durable-instruction.test.ts`, `knowledge-convergence-owner-publish.test.ts`.
- **A deadline that lapsed while a cycle ran** (the supervision startup grace outlasted by a slow first cycle, or an expired mandate beside a later check). Prevented: every duty reports its deadlines as they are, a lapsed one gets its one extra cycle, and it never hides a later deadline. Before this, the supervision owner reported its startup grace only while it lay in the future, so an owner whose first cycle outlasted the 60-second grace never woke for its deferred evaluation and stayed `pending` until an unrelated event; on 2026-11-21 that made a WSL Node fail readiness twice and roll back v0.30.0. → `conversation-change-loop.test.ts`, `supervision-change-loop.test.ts`, `outcome-supervision-runtime.test.ts`.
- **A dormant supervision owner.** Prevented from polling: an inactive workstream is settled, woken by an event or by the current-workstream and branch-binding files; workforce sources are watched too. → `supervision-change-loop.test.ts`, `supervision-wake-sources.test.ts`.
- **An idle replica peer stream.** Prevented: a quiet stream writes nothing and arms no timer. SSH keepalives end a dead link, and that EOF is the only failure; a stopped peer process that keeps the link open does no harm while nothing is pending, and the next transfer's command deadline reports it once work arrives. → `repository-replica-ssh-subscription.test.ts`, `repository-replica-watch.test.ts`.
- **A held Node resident waiting for activation.** Recovered when the writer coordinate changes. → `knowledge-worker-admission.test.ts`.
- **A CLI host checkout held on an outdated writer.** Recovered on the signal that clears its blocker: the writer coordinate, the runtime pointer and the fleet registry are watched; only a blocker a remote reader must clear (a Node or Hub on an earlier release) is retried, from 30 minutes doubling to 8 hours. → `knowledge-host-activation-retry.test.ts`, `knowledge-host-residents.test.ts`.

## Liveness on demand (step 3)

A process proves it runs by holding a local presence socket (Unix: a path socket in the user's private state directory, or, when that is too deep for a socket path, in a short directory under the home directory; Windows: a named pipe). The directory must be this user's and closed to everyone else, or the presence is refused. A token names its host, boot, pid, nonce and a namespace key: the network and pid namespaces and the directory by its device and inode, so a process that sees the same path on another filesystem (a container sharing the host's pids and network) reads the holder as unknown, never gone. The directory is never removed, since a new one would have another inode. A probe connects and hangs up; a waiter keeps a connection, which the kernel closes the moment the holder exits or releases; one byte over a connection wakes the holder. Nothing is rewritten on a timer.

- **A resident, a held resident or a CLI host that died.** Visible at the next read: its record names its presence, which no longer answers, so it reads as not running; a record of an earlier release, which has none, keeps its heartbeat rule. A missing socket is gone, and so is a reset connection (Linux resets one still pending when a listener closes) and a refused connection on Linux, where a full backlog answers EAGAIN instead. Elsewhere (macOS) a full backlog also refuses, so a refusal is gone only once the pid is gone too; an exit wait raises `process_exit_wait_unavailable` once refusals from a live pid last, which a stuck process or a stale socket of a reused pid causes. Any other error proves nothing. → `resident-health.test.ts`, `host-resident-status.test.ts`, `process-presence.test.ts` (a `SIGKILL`ed holder, and a refusal from a live process).
- **A presence this host cannot judge** (another namespace, state directory, host or boot). Never read as gone: a probe answers unknown, and a health reader falls back to the writer's pid. A standby CLI host that cannot see its lock holder raises `process_exit_wait_unavailable` on each repository it would serve, and rescans when the lock file changes. → `process-presence.test.ts`, `resident-health.test.ts`, `knowledge-host-residents.test.ts`.
- **A stopped or hung consumer** (S3). A stopped process still holds its presence, and that is correct while no work is pending: nothing waits on it, so it does no harm. Once work arrives, every commit pings its event channel and records its commit time, and a subscriber that has not observed an unobserved commit within 30 seconds is reported as stalled. → `change-channel.test.ts` (a `SIGSTOP`ped subscriber).
- **A waiter behind an admission holder.** Recovered at once: a release wakes every waiter's presence; a crashed holder's or a crashed waiter-ahead's exit closes the waiter's held connection; a live holder that stopped renewing is replaced at its lease's exact expiry. Only an earlier release's holder or waiter, or lock contention, falls back to a bounded retry, which a known holder's lease expiry still ends, which grows only after a wait that used it, and which starts over when the holder changes (ADR-0606). An exit wait whose connection fails for a reason that proves nothing retries from a second, doubling to five minutes, and raises `process_exit_wait_unavailable` on the repository while it cannot see its process. → `full-cycle-admission.test.ts` (a release and a killed holder, each with a one-minute retry interval), `process-presence.test.ts`.
- **Mixed versions on one host.** Safe while both run: an earlier release removes rows by heartbeat age, so while one of its holders, waiters or residents is present, this release's waiter keeps retrying and its resident rewrites its health row every minute; that work exists only while an earlier release runs. An earlier-release waiter that arrives after this one sleeps is seen at this waiter's next signal; at worst it goes ahead once, and admission never has two holders. → `full-cycle-admission.test.ts`, `resident-health.test.ts`.
- **An upgrade waiting behind a running cycle.** Recovered at once: the upgrade wakes the holder's presence once per holder, also when lock contention hid the holder at first, which makes a running provider call check for it instead of polling. → `upgrade-resident-exclusion.test.ts`, `upgrade-preemption.test.ts`, `full-cycle-admission.test.ts`.
- **A standby CLI host.** Recovered at once: it waits on the holder's presence and takes the lock when it closes. → `knowledge-host-residents.test.ts`.
- **The owner lease.** Kept live while its holder holds its Hub `knowledge` wait, whatever its recorded expiry, so an idle owner renews nothing; that extension is never stored. Decisions report `holderPresent`, and a waiting host arms no expiry deadline. When the holder leaves, the Hub moves the stored expiry, the last one the holder was given, out to a minute later if it was sooner, never earlier: a drop the holder did not see leaves it able to commit until its own validity ends, so no peer is granted the lease before then. The other hosts are woken and take over at that exact expiry, or at once on a release. A standalone owner arms no renewal; a Hub owner renews only while a cycle runs or its Hub wait is down. → `knowledge-change-wait-http.test.ts`, `convergence-owner-presence.test.ts`, `loop-owner-deadline.test.ts`.
- **An earlier Hub** (before this release, such as 0.23.7). Visible and safe: it rejects the `knowledge` wait, so `hub_knowledge_changes_unavailable` is raised and the wait retries with backoff to five minutes; the owner renews its lease and a waiting host wakes at its expiry, as before. The Hub is deployed before Nodes and hosts.
- **A presence that cannot be held** (a directory others can enter or another user owns, an unusable path). Visible: its token is never recorded, `process_presence_unavailable` is raised with the fix until it is released, and readers keep the earlier rules. → `process-presence.test.ts`, `held-presence.test.ts`.
