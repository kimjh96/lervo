# Execution and completion

1. Bind the interpreted request and acceptance criteria to the workstream and owner.
2. Produce exact assignment plans and worker responsibility bindings; admit authorized effects.
3. Record handoff; separately observe acknowledgment and actual attempt start.
4. Consume start, completion, failure, deadline, cancellation, and authority-change evidence. Dispatch never ends delegation responsibility.
5. Retry only known-safe failures within policy. Inspect uncertain effects before replay. Reassign or escalate when local recovery is exhausted.
6. Review artifact contents and verification against acceptance criteria; request bounded rework. Lint, synchronization, checkpoint updates, and provider exit alone do not establish acceptance.
7. Complete the canonical goal only after acceptance and consistent assignment state. Deliver the result; retry failed publication independently of completed work.

Reports lead with product progress, artifacts, findings, and decisions. Internal lifecycle details remain inspectable evidence. Reserve human decisions for material ambiguity or authority beyond the installed mandate. Routine allocation and safe recovery do not require repeated approval.
