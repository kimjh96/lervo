# Local decision attention

`work.owner_attention.v1` records one concrete decision need against the owner's exact semantic task basis. It uses `work.supervision.escalation.recorded` with the local owner as recipient, a bounded reason code, the exact decision-plan artifact hash, and no scheduled retry. The rationale artifact states the missing decision. This records local follow-up responsibility without claiming external delivery or central acceptance.

An unchanged basis waits across restart without another model call. A canonical goal, staff, task, review, or relevant peer change invalidates the basis and permits fresh planning. Existing authority and escalation limits still apply. Publication remains a separate receipt-backed operation.

An attention decision for exhausted retry authority remains pending after a validity-only mandate renewal and workstream bookkeeping update when all of these conditions hold:

- Its exact receipt is confirmed.
- The prior mandate and workstream snapshots are readable.
- The substantive mandate scope and goal purpose are unchanged.
- No intervening task event exists.
- A known-safe failed assignment still exhausts the current recovery limit.

Raising the attempt limit, changing a material authority or goal field, or recording another effect releases this narrow continuity proof for fresh planning. The old attention does not grant a retry, reset usage, or claim delivery.
