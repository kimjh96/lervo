# Local decision attention

`work.owner_attention.v1` records a concrete decision need against the owner's exact semantic task basis. It uses `work.supervision.escalation.recorded` with the local owner as recipient, a bounded reason code, the exact decision-plan artifact hash, and no scheduled retry. The rationale artifact states the missing decision. This records local follow-up responsibility without claiming external delivery or central acceptance.

An unchanged basis waits across restart without another model call. A canonical goal, staff, task, review, or relevant peer change invalidates the basis and permits fresh planning. Existing authority and escalation limits still apply. Publication remains a separate receipt-backed operation.

`owner_proposal_invalid` is the bounded local-attention reason when an owner model result has malformed escalation, support-request, or support-acceptance detail fields, or mixes incompatible effect variants. The exact result remains in private CAS, linked by hash from a bounded string rationale. Neither the raw result nor optional explanatory fields enter the event or health projection. This state prevents repeated processing of the same poisoned result; it does not claim delivery of the missing user decision or completion of the underlying goal.

An attention decision for exhausted retry authority remains pending after a validity-only mandate renewal and workstream bookkeeping update only when all conditions hold:

- Its exact receipt is confirmed.
- The prior mandate and workstream snapshots are readable.
- The substantive mandate scope and goal purpose are unchanged.
- No intervening task event exists.
- A known-safe failed assignment still exhausts the current recovery limit.

Raising the attempt limit, changing a material authority or goal field, or recording another effect releases this narrow continuity proof for fresh planning. The old attention does not grant a retry, reset usage, or claim delivery.
