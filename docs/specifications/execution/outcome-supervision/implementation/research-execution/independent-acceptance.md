# Independent acceptance

## Evidence and semantic judgement

The Node reconstructs research evidence from the producer's ordered input-binding, adapter-start, verification-completion, and run-completion events. It reads exact input, result, verification, and stdout originals and recomputes schema and traceability checks. A successful process exit or suite name alone is insufficient.

Owner reasoning receives the captured source texts and returned brief. Its `prepare_completion` verdict adds `researchChecks`, each with exactly `checkId` and `status`. Required semantic checks preserve citation meaning and require direct support for each major conclusion. The evaluator runs separately from the producer and cannot replace failed checks with deterministic success.

The same exact source, input, result and checker originals remain available to the owner after assignment completion. The reader supplies the recomputed verification report, each captured source's measured SHA-256 and byte count against its source-policy limit, a fresh comparison between captured and current source hashes, and the producer run's exact adapter-start event with its execution mode and effective file-change capability. These observations have separate meanings: the fresh hash proves equality at observation time, while the adapter event records the managed execution boundary; neither alone proves that no transient write ever occurred. A completed assignment does not erase research evidence before the separate artifact review. If a prior `blocked` review lacked those originals, its verdict remains historical; the owner must examine the newly available canonical evidence and record a separate admitted review before acceptance.

## Completion admission and replay

`work.assignment_completion.v2` adds `roleEvaluationArtifactHash` to the existing completion effect. The artifact uses the established role-evaluation contract, combining exact deterministic originals and the independently recorded owner result.

Completion admission and replay reconstruct the evaluation, check its distinct producer and evaluator runs, and compare its exact CAS hash. Research completion requires a passed role evaluation as well as the writing permit. A failed semantic judgement produces an exact rework review with its recorded findings and leaves the assignment incomplete.

## Source-policy readiness

Source-policy readiness reads actual task-scoped sources. Stable policy and source hashes enter owner facts, so a repaired or changed policy invalidates an old attention basis. Missing policy remains unobserved. Readiness does not claim execution or publication.
