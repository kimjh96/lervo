# Contextual user communication

User-facing replies must express relevant outcomes naturally in the conversation's language. Never append an unconditional repository-change footer, file list, checkpoint revision, lint result, replica status, or telemetry summary.

The native-work renderer applies platform length and punctuation handling to the provider's contextual response without adding a repository-change footer. Its instructions request relevant observed outcomes in the conversation language.

The separate remote-command result publisher preserves the verified response without an unconditional completion preface. Prepared-outbound replay retries transport independently, retaining the same result and writing evaluation across restart. The explicit supervision factory connects canonical accepted-goal results to an independently recoverable [publication queue](implementation/result-delivery.md). Queueing and a transport receipt remain separate evidence.

## Relevance and evidence

Keep structured change and execution observations in canonical evidence independently of presentation. Include a fact in prose when it answers the request, changes the user's understanding of completion, explains a material limitation, or identifies a necessary decision. Detailed paths and operational observations remain available when requested or useful for review.

Ordinary questions and successful research answers need no statement that repository files did not change. A requested fix that made no change must explain the actual outcome. Removing the footer must not hide a failed save or unverified result. Observed absence of file changes never means no work, no state change, or no external effect.

Generate the final answer from the verified outcome available to the writer. Validate factual claims and material omissions against that evidence. Correct unsupported completion claims or state uncertainty explicitly; do not append a contradictory stock disclaimer. Avoid duplicate summaries of facts already covered.

These examples illustrate the contract, not replacement templates:

- Research reports findings and sources.
- A successful fix explains changed behavior and relevant verification.
- A persistence failure explains what could not be saved and its effect on the requested result.

## Rendering and completion boundaries

Rendering owns platform length and escaping. The shared writing contract owns relevance, language, and evidence fidelity. Do not add an unconditional extra model call solely to rewrite a footer.

The native-work completion path observes changed paths and passes them to lifecycle verification and canonical completion evidence independently of the rendered response. Durable duplicate delivery reuses the recorded result without rerunning work.

This presentation change does not independently validate every natural-language factual claim. Full supervision acceptance and material-omission validation remain part of production delivery work.
