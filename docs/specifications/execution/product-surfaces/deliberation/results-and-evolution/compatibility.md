# Compatibility and evolution

Version-1 deliberation artifacts, outcomes, events, and queue records remain readable without reinterpretation. New artifact events retain original summary fields and optionally add the complete validated structured artifact as a source-free projection source. They may include the policy-bound participant name for presentation. Predecessors without that name use honest role labeling, never an invented agent identity. Every duplicate coordinate must match.

Legacy events without the structured artifact remain valid but cannot independently reconstruct complete claim, objection, verification, and decision semantics. Product routes and `ProductDeliberationViewV1` are additive.

Future budget dimensions, principal kinds, artifact kinds, or terminal meanings require explicit new versions, current-and-predecessor fixtures, restart and rollback coverage, and historical replay.
