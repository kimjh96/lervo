# Reconcile all related open work before ending a turn

Before ending any turn, check every open plan item, every earlier owner directive, every finding marked for later, and every Lervo health or attention line related to the work. Act on each one or record why it is deferred. Do not limit work to the single item the owner named when related items in the same plan remain open, and do not wait for the owner to point out missed work.
