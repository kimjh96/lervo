# Hold every Lervo ADE surface to one finished, consistent craft standard

Hold every Lervo ADE surface to one finished, consistent craft standard, not only menus and dialogs: floating surfaces are glass (the floating tint over a backdrop blur, the window-edge light, the window shadow), never opaque panels outlined with lines; surfaces on the window glass are separated by fill and light, not drawn boxes; a modal blurs what is behind it; controls in one group share one height, corner, and type, with primary and secondary differing only in fill; actions are named, never guessed from one field; locations show the folder name and change through the system folder panel, never as truncated path text; menus hang directly under the control that opened them, aligned and as wide; windows sit centered; nothing appears and then moves because data arrived later; and every overlay keeps its motion visible as the motion policy says. Write these as rules in the ADE design system and check every change against them in the real app, frame by frame, in light and dark.
