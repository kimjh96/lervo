# ADE space transitions collapse and expand like a disclosure

In the Lervo ADE, anything that takes up space and then leaves must give its space back smoothly, collapsing like a disclosure, instead of the space vanishing at once; anything that arrives opens its space the same way. This applies everywhere in the ADE, including the sidebar and the conversation, and the ADE motion policy must state it.
