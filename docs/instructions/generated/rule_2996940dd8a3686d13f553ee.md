# Lervo maintenance agents share coordination but are marked by origin and hidden from the team view

Lervo maintenance agents (knowledge convergence and practice workers, their delegation parent, the Knowledge Steward) stay agents in the same coordination model as the team, so leases and evidence keep the team and Lervo from overwriting each other, but each records its origin as lervo instead of being guessed from names. They do not count toward the team headcount or the registry bound, retired worker records are pruned to a bound, and the ADE sidebar shows only the team; Lervo agents surface only when they stop or need the owner.
