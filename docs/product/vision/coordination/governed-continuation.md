# Governed continuation

After foundation readiness, Lervo projects current assignments and managed-run evidence through a provider-neutral headless application contract. A governed loop may recommend claim, attempt, bounded retry, wait, block, escalation, or completion. Canonical authority remains with workstreams, assignments, leases, events, verification, and repository state.

One explicitly authorized bounded foreground scheduler may poll and execute those decisions without repeated manual triggers. It grants neither implicit approval nor in-flight preemption.

A separate reviewed topology boundary may add bounded branch-and-join nodes to the current assignment graph. It binds the exact graph and role contracts, creates deterministic blocked assignments before activation, and retains ordinary dependency, lease, execution, and verification authority. It cannot rewire or delete existing nodes.

Separately installed product hosts discover the versioned local boundary through `lervo application capabilities --json`. The packed CLI protocol exposes workforce, operating, and collaboration routes without publishing storage internals or granting product UI, database, Hub, implicit approval, or connector authority. Protocol v5 describes bounded scheduler authority; protocol v6 adds graph-v2 inspection and exact reviewed topology changes.
