# Users, organization, and product surfaces

Lervo supports developers, researchers, designers, product operators, marketers, and other repository-based roles. Role profiles may differ but share one lifecycle for context, capability decisions, evidence, review, and handoff.

One Lervo organization may coordinate multiple repository-backed project teams. Hired agents are durable expert identities rather than disposable provider sessions. A policy-bound central leadership agent may coordinate teams without replacing the human owner or weakening repository authority.

Discord is the current installed reference product surface, not the organization runtime or source of truth. Lervo-owned capabilities must be usable and verifiable there before a future first-party surface replaces or coexists with it.

Both surfaces use the same headless Lervo application contracts and preserve hired-agent identity, reviewed context, conversation coordinates, work state, authority decisions, evidence, source lifecycle, canonical knowledge, and observability.
