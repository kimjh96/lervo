# Coordination and governed continuation

## Optional coordination

A Hub may synchronize allowed events, route signed commands, and show cross-project status. A project node revalidates identity, authorization, freshness, repository state, and local policy before execution.

## Governed continuation

After foundation readiness, Lervo projects current assignments and managed-run evidence through a provider-neutral headless application contract. A governed loop may recommend claim, attempt, bounded retry, wait, block, escalation, or completion. Canonical authority remains with workstreams, assignments, leases, events, verification, and repository state.

One explicitly authorized bounded foreground scheduler may poll and execute those decisions without repeated manual triggers. It grants neither implicit approval, in-flight preemption, nor dynamic graph mutation.

Separately installed product hosts discover the versioned local boundary through `lervo application capabilities --json`. The packed CLI protocol exposes workforce, operating, and collaboration routes without publishing storage internals or granting product UI, database, Hub, implicit approval, dynamic graph, or connector authority. Protocol v5 describes the scheduler's explicit bounded execution authority.
