Needs the Lervo CLI 0.43.0 or later.

## What's changed

- Keep the header title and the first message steady while started work's workspace arrives.
- Paste images and screenshots into the reply card and the instruction of new work; messages show them as images, and each opens at full size.
- Show a compaction as a thin divider, and mark a reply sent while the agent works as queued until its next step.
- Show messages from other agent sessions framed and named in the conversation.
- Show turn notifications on macOS: ask the system's permission at the first one, and sign the app as dev.lervo.ade so the notification daemon serves it.
